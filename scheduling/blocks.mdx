---
title: Blocking times
icon: ban
---

Sometimes you can't take appointments — a holiday, a day off, or someone on your
team is away. **Blocks** let you close those times so visitors can't book them.

<Frame caption="Unavailable days: block vacations or days off and the wizard hides those dates.">
  <img src="/images/scheduling/agenda-blocks-en.webp" alt="Linkzee screenshot" />
</Frame>

When a time is blocked, it simply stops being offered in the booking wizard. Your
regular business hours stay the same; the block just carves out the times you're
unavailable.

## Two kinds of blocks

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Business block" icon="calendar-xmark">
    Closes the whole agenda — nobody can book. Use it for a holiday or a day when
    you shut down completely.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Staff block" icon="user-xmark">
    Closes one person's times — everyone else stays bookable. Use it for a team
    member's vacation or a sick day.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  A **staff block** only affects the person you choose. The rest of your team keeps
  taking appointments as usual.
</Note>

Once a block is in place, the affected times disappear from your visitors' options
right away, and your live page updates automatically.
