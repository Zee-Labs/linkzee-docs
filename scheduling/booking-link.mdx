Sometimes you want people to skip everything else and go straight to booking.
A **booking link** does exactly that: it opens your Linkzee page **right in the
booking wizard**, styled in your theme, so visitors can pick a time in a couple
of taps.

## Your booking link

Your link looks like this:

```
linkz.ee/your-name?agenda=1
```

It's the same public page as `linkz.ee/your-name`, but it lands visitors in the
scheduling step from the start — perfect for an Instagram bio, a WhatsApp
message, or anywhere you want a one-tap way to book.

## Copy your link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Scheduling settings">
    Go to **Scheduling** and open its settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the booking link">
    Find the booking link and click **Copy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Paste it wherever your audience is — your Instagram or TikTok bio, a
    WhatsApp broadcast, or a post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Everything auto-saves, so your booking link always reflects your latest
  services and hours — no need to update the link when you change your schedule.
</Note>

## Pre-select a service

You can share a link that already has a service chosen, so visitors skip the
service-selection step and go straight to picking a time. It's a smooth way to
promote one specific offer.
