If people work with you, Linkzee keeps the commission math for you: a rule per
professional, a running statement, and a **close period** button for when you
settle up.

<Note>
  Linkzee **records** commissions — it doesn't move money. Payment to your team
  happens the way it already does today (transfer, cash, payroll). What changes
  is that you stop doing this math in a spreadsheet.
</Note>

## Set the rule for each professional

Go to **Scheduling → Team** and open a professional:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Earns commission">
    The toggle enables commission for that person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose percentage or fixed amount">
    **Percentage** of the service price, or a **fixed amount** per appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the value">
    A percentage between 0 and 100, or an amount in your currency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The base is always the **full service price** — the value on your services
list, before any discount.

### Services that don't pay commission

Some services shouldn't enter the math at all. In **Scheduling → Services**,
each service has an **Earns commission** toggle. Turn it off and that
service stays out of the commission base for **everyone** on the team.

## When a commission is counted

A commission accrues when the appointment is **completed** — not when it is
booked, and not when the client pays.

That timing is deliberate: it is the moment the work actually happened, so it
behaves the same whether the client paid online, paid in person, or didn't pay
you through Linkzee at all.

If a completed appointment is later **refunded** or turned into a **no-show**,
the commission is **reversed** automatically — including when the period was
already closed, where it comes back as a negative entry so your next settlement
is correct.

## Follow the totals

The **Team commissions** card lives on your **Earnings** page. Pick a window — 7,
30 or 90 days — and you get, per professional:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Accrued and not settled yet |
| **Settled** | Already closed in a previous settlement |
| **Entries** | How many appointments generated commission |

Open a professional to see every entry: date, service, client, base price,
commission and status (**Open**, **Settled** or **Reversed**).

## Settle the period

When you pay someone, click **Settle period**. Every open entry in that window
is locked as **settled**, so the next period starts clean and you always know
what has already been paid.

<Warning>
  Settling a period is a bookkeeping action — it doesn't send money. Pay your
  professional the way you normally do, then settle the period to record it.
</Warning>

## What your team sees

Professionals you invited (see [Team](/scheduling/team)) get their own area
with **My schedule** and **My commissions** — their own entries only. They
never see other professionals' numbers or your store's finances.
