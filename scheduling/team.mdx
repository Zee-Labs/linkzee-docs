Add the people who take appointments to your agenda. Once you have staff,
visitors can pick **who** they want to book with before choosing a time.

## Add a staff member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Scheduling">
    Go to **Scheduling** and open the **Team** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the person">
    Click **+ Add** and fill in their details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their services">
    Pick which of your services this person offers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For each staff member you set:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The name visitors see when they pick who to book with. |
| **Title** | Their role, like "Barber" or "Nail artist". |
| **Photo** | A profile picture that shows on the booking card. |
| **Services** | The services this person can take bookings for. |
| **Own hours** | Optional. Their personal availability. |

## Own hours

Each person can have their **own hours**. If you leave them empty, that person
simply inherits your business hours. Set them when someone works a different
schedule from the rest of the team — see [Business hours](/scheduling/business-hours).

## From solo to team

Adding your **first** staff member turns a solo agenda into a **Team** agenda —
no migration and no extra setup. Everything you already created keeps working;
visitors just gain the step of choosing who to book with.

<Note>
  Everything you change here auto-saves and the preview updates live — on the
  right on desktop, and below the editor on mobile.
</Note>

## Give each professional their own area

A professional can have their **own login** in Linkzee — with their schedule
and their commissions, and nothing else from your business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    On the professional's card, click **Invite** and enter their email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    They receive an email with a link that is valid for **7 days**, and only an
    account with **that same email** can accept it. If they don't have a Linkzee
    account yet, they create one right there — the account is theirs, free, and
    has no page of its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They're in">
    From then on, they open **Teams** in their own dashboard and switch to your
    business whenever they want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While the invite is pending, the card shows an **Invited** badge. You can
cancel an invite and send a new one at any time — the previous link stops
working.

### What they see (and what they don't)

| They see | They never see |
| --- | --- |
| Their own upcoming appointments | Other professionals' appointments |
| Their own commissions and statement | Your revenue, fees or payouts |
| Client name and service for their appointments | Your page editor, settings or plan |

Times always show in **your business's timezone**, so there is no confusion
when someone lives in another region.

### Ending someone's access

**Remove access** takes the person out of your business immediately — but it
**does not delete the professional**. Their history, appointments and
commission statement stay exactly where they are, and they keep read access to
their own statement.

<Note>
  Access is checked on every request, so revoking is immediate — there is no
  window where a former team member still gets in.
</Note>

## Slots mode

If your agenda runs in **Slots** mode — for courts, group classes or anything
with several spots per time — you set a **capacity** per time instead of adding
named staff. Each time slot then accepts that many bookings.
