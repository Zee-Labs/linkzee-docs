Turn any service into a paid booking: ask for a **deposit** or the **full
amount** at the moment the visitor books. The money goes **straight to you**
through Stripe, Linkzee's payments partner — Linkzee never holds your funds.

## Activate payments

1. Open **Earnings** in the dashboard menu and find the **Enable payments** card.
2. Tap **Activate payments**. You'll be taken to **Stripe** to create your
   secure payment account with its own login — this happens **only the first
   time**. Have your business or personal details and your bank account handy.
3. When you finish, the card shows **Active** — and your balance and payouts
   appear right there on the **Earnings** page.

<Note>
  Your Stripe account is yours: you own the balance, the payout schedule and
  the full Stripe dashboard. If Stripe needs an extra verification later, a
  banner appears on the Earnings page — and a dismissible warning shows across
  the dashboard — so you can resolve it without leaving Linkzee.
</Note>

## Choose what each service charges

Open a service in **Scheduling → Services** and pick its **Online charging**:

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Don't charge** | Booking works exactly as before — no payment step. |
| **Deposit** | The visitor pays a part upfront — a **percentage** of the price or a **fixed amount** you set. |
| **Full amount** | The visitor pays the whole service price to confirm. |

## What your visitor sees

When a service charges, a **payment step** appears at the end of booking:

1. The time slot is **held for 20 minutes** while they pay.
2. They pay by **card** — or **PIX**, when you have PIX enabled in your Stripe
   payment settings (Brazil).
3. The booking is **confirmed the moment the payment goes through** — that's
   when confirmations, reminders and the Google Meet link (if any) are sent.

If they don't finish paying, the hold expires after 20 minutes and the slot
opens up again automatically — no dead slots.

## Refunds

When a booking with payment is cancelled **within your cancellation policy**
(by the visitor or by you), the payment is **refunded automatically in full**
— including the Linkzee fee.

## Fees

Stripe charges its standard processing fee per transaction (shown in every
payout). On top of that, Linkzee keeps a small fee per booking payment,
depending on your plan:

| Plan | Linkzee fee per booking payment |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5% |
| Essential | 3% |
| Pro | 1.5% |
| **Scale** | **0%** |

You always see the exact fee amounts in **Earnings**, right in the dashboard
menu — nothing is hidden. See [Earnings & payouts](/scheduling/earnings).
