A **confirmation reminder** is a message sent to your visitor a few hours before
their appointment. It nudges them to show up — and lets them confirm with a single
tap — so you lose fewer bookings to no-shows.

## Set up your reminder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how far ahead">
    Pick how many hours before the appointment the reminder goes out: **1**, **2**,
    **3**, **6**, **12**, **24** or **48** hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the channels">
    Send the reminder by **email**, by **WhatsApp**, or both. Email reminders
    are included with scheduling (Pro and Scale); **WhatsApp reminders are a
    Scale feature**. Both follow the plan of **this store** — see
    [Plans & pricing](/billing/plans) and
    [Plan per store](/billing/plan-per-store).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything auto-saves, so your choices apply to new bookings right away.

## What the message includes

The reminder message carries a **confirm** button so your visitor can lock in the
appointment in one tap. For online services, it also includes the **Meet link**, so
they can join the meeting straight from the reminder.

It also gives them a **"Can't make it"** option. If your visitor taps it, the
appointment is cancelled and the time slot is **freed up automatically** — so
someone else can grab it, instead of a silent no-show blocking your agenda. You're
notified when this happens.

<Tip>
  When a visitor confirms their reminder, Linkzee can **check the appointment off as
  done automatically** once the time has passed — see the [Scheduling
  dashboard](/scheduling/dashboard).
</Tip>

<Note>
  The reminder only goes out if the booking is further away than your chosen window.
  Appointments booked inside that window are picked up in the next cycle.
</Note>
