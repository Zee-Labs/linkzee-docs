If your business already runs its appointments on **Qzee**, you don't need to set
up a second calendar. The **Qzee agenda** block puts a booking button at the top
of your bio that opens your Qzee agenda right on the page. Clients book straight
on Qzee: your services, hours, team and booking rules are the ones you already
set there.

It fits any business that books on Qzee: a salon, a clinic, a studio, a
consultant.

## Before you start

- You need a **Qzee account with online booking turned on**.
- Keep at hand the link to your Qzee booking page (for example
  `qzee.io/book/my-unit`) or the unit code.

<Note>
  Your bio takes bookings through **one agenda only**. When the Qzee agenda goes
  on your bio, the Linkzee agenda leaves it. You can switch back at any time.
</Note>

## Add the Qzee agenda

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add menu">
    On your **Links** screen, click **+ Add**. Under **Integrations**, pick
    **Qzee agenda**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your unit">
    Tap **I already use Qzee**, paste the link to your Qzee booking page or the
    unit code in **Unit link or code**, and tap **Connect**. Linkzee checks the
    agenda on Qzee before connecting. Don't have Qzee yet? **Learn about Qzee**
    opens its website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the switch (only if the Linkzee agenda is on your bio)">
    If your bio already takes bookings through the Linkzee agenda, you see
    **Switch to the Qzee agenda** with what stays in place: the upcoming
    appointments and the clients with package credit or a subscription. Tap
    **Confirm the switch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The block appears in **Links** as a card marked **Pinned to top**: it sits
right below your header, so it doesn't drag like a link. The card shows how
many times it was opened and has a switch to show or hide the agenda on your
bio. Open it to **Change unit** or **Disconnect**.

### What happens to the Linkzee agenda

Nothing is deleted. The Linkzee agenda only stops taking **new** bookings from
your bio:

- The booking card, the services table and the packages showcase leave your
  bio while Qzee is on.
- Appointments already booked stay valid, with reminders, the management link
  and the [customer area](/scheduling/customer-area).
- Clients with package credit or a subscription keep booking through the
  customer area and the management link.
- Your [AI assistant](/scheduling/ai-scheduling) no longer offers times. It
  points visitors to the Qzee agenda, and it can still list, reschedule and
  cancel Linkzee agenda bookings.

To go back, open the Linkzee agenda card in **Links** and tap **Back to the
Linkzee agenda**. The Qzee card leaves your bio, and the Qzee connection stays
saved for later.

### If the card shows a warning

The Qzee card tells you when something on the Qzee side needs attention:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The online agenda is not taking bookings right now | Turn online booking back on for that unit in Qzee. |
| The agenda has no service or hours set up on Qzee yet | Add services and hours in Qzee. |
| We can no longer find this agenda on Qzee | Check the link, or use **Change unit**. |
| We could not check the agenda on Qzee right now | Tap **Check again** in a moment. |
| The **Allow embedding** option is off on Qzee | The button still works, but it opens the agenda in a full page. Turn the option on in Qzee to keep clients on your bio. |

## What visitors see

At the top of your bio, in the same spot and with the same look as the booking
button, visitors see a button with the title you gave your booking button, or
**Book an appointment** if you didn't set one.

Tapping it opens your Qzee agenda in a panel over your bio, with your page's
colors, fonts and language. The visitor picks the service, the time and
confirms, all without leaving your page. If the browser can't show the agenda
there, it opens in a full page instead.

After booking, the same device shows **Your next appointment** above the button
and **My bookings** inside the card, just like the Linkzee agenda. **My
bookings** opens the [customer area](/scheduling/customer-area), where the
client can reschedule or cancel.

Your [booking link](/scheduling/booking-link) keeps working: it takes visitors
to the Qzee button.

## Privacy

The booking is made on Qzee, and Linkzee doesn't store it. What lets a client
see and change a booking stays on their own device, in a separate and isolated
Linkzee area (`vault.linkz.ee`). It never sits on your bio page, so no script on
the page, including your own tracking tags, can reach it.

Your bio only remembers that the device has booked with you and a short reminder
of the next appointment, so it can show **Your next appointment**.

## Measuring results

Each time a visitor opens the Qzee card, it counts as an opening. Your own
visits don't count. You see the openings on the card in **Links** and in
[Analytics](/analytics/overview), where the card appears as **Qzee agenda** in
the button ranking.

Bookings show up in your **Qzee report**. Openings and bookings measure
different things, so the two numbers don't compare.
