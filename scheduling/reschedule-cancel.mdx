Plans change, and Linkzee makes it easy to move or drop an appointment. Both
**you** and the **visitor** can reschedule or cancel a booking, and everyone
stays in the loop automatically.

## How visitors reschedule or cancel

Visitors don't need an account. On your page, they open **My appointments** in
the booking wizard to find their booking, then reschedule it to a new time or
cancel it. They can also just ask your AI assistant to do it for them in the
chat — see [AI scheduling](/scheduling/ai-scheduling).

Every booking also has its own **manage page** — its link travels in the
confirmation email and on the confirmation screen. The page opens in your
page's own theme and shows the booking details, the **Google Meet link** for
online services and, for paid bookings, the **receipt**. From there the
visitor can **Reschedule** or tap **Cancel appointment**.

## How you reschedule or cancel

You manage everything from your **schedule**. Open the appointment you want to
change, move it to a new time, or cancel it — whichever the situation calls for.

## What happens after a change

When an appointment is rescheduled, the visitor gets an **email with the new
date and time**. For an **online service**, that email also includes the
updated **Google Meet link**, so they always have the right details in one
place.

When a booking is **cancelled**, the visitor gets a cancellation email. If
the booking was **paid**, that email also explains the **automatic refund** —
and the manage page shows **Refund in progress** until it becomes **Payment
refunded**, so nobody is left wondering about the money.

<Note>
  Everything you change on the schedule saves automatically, and your live page
  updates in real time — there's no Save button to remember.
</Note>

## The cancellation window

Your **cancellation window** controls how late a visitor may change a booking.
Set it in your booking rules — once the window has passed, visitors can no
longer reschedule or cancel on their own, so last-minute changes stay in your
hands. You can still move or cancel any appointment yourself at any time.
