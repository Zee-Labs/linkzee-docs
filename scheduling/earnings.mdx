The **Earnings** tab in Scheduling is your financial overview: what came in,
from whom, by which payment method, for which service — with charts, a
searchable list of transactions, and your payouts right below.

<Frame caption="Earnings — ① period totals, ② what you received per day.">

</Frame>

## The numbers

Pick a window — **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** — and you'll see:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Received" icon="wallet">
    Total paid by clients in the period, minus refunds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refunds" icon="rotate-left">
    Amounts returned to clients (cancellations within your policy).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments" icon="circle-check">
    How many booking payments happened.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average ticket" icon="chart-line">
    Received divided by the number of payments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Below the cards: a **daily chart** of what you received, a breakdown by
**payment method** (card, PIX…), and your **top services** by revenue.

## Transactions

Every payment, one per line: client, service, payment method, date and amount
(refunds show in a different color). Use the search box to find a payment by
**name, phone, email or service**.

## Fee transparency

If your plan has a Linkzee fee on booking payments, the exact amount and
percentage for the period is shown right under the numbers — and on the
**Scale** plan that fee is **0%**. See the fee table in
[Charge for bookings](/scheduling/payments#fees).

## Payouts — how the money reaches your bank

The **Payouts** section at the bottom explains the money flow and shows the
official numbers, straight from Stripe:

1. **Client pays** — the amount lands instantly in your Stripe balance.
2. **Transparent deductions** — Stripe deducts its processing fee, plus the
   Linkzee fee of your plan (when there is one).
3. **It hits your bank** — payouts are automatic, on the standard schedule for
   your country and payment method (PIX settles faster than cards).

Each payout in the list shows the **exact net amount** that reaches your bank,
with every deduction itemized — the same official statement you'd see in
Stripe.

<Note>
  The dashboard numbers are the gross amounts processed through bookings.
  Stripe's processing fees are deducted at payout — the payouts list is where
  you see the final net values.
</Note>
