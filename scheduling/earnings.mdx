**Earnings** is a top-level item in your dashboard menu — one page with your
whole financial overview: what came in, from whom, by which payment method,
for which service — with charts, a searchable list of transactions, and your
payouts right below.

<Note>
  Earnings used to be a tab inside Scheduling. It's now its own page in the
  main menu — the old tab simply points you here.
</Note>

When a booking payment is confirmed, Linkzee also sends you a push
notification — **"💰 You received \{amount\}"** — and tapping it opens this
page. See [Push notifications](/account/notifications).

## The numbers

Pick a window — **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** — and you'll see:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Received" icon="wallet">
    Total paid by clients in the period, minus refunds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refunds" icon="rotate-left">
    Amounts returned to clients (cancellations within your policy).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments" icon="circle-check">
    How many booking payments happened.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average ticket" icon="chart-line">
    Received divided by the number of payments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Below the cards: a **daily chart** of what you received, a breakdown by
**payment method** (card, PIX…), and your **top services** by revenue.

## Transactions

The list reads like a bank statement. A payment shows as a **positive** line
— client, service, payment method, date and amount. Besides booking payments,
the list also includes **package sales** and **subscription charges**, each
labeled as such (see [Session packages](/scheduling/packages) and
[Recurring plans](/scheduling/subscription-plans)). When a payment is
refunded, the original line stays and a second, **negative** line labeled
**Refund** appears on the refund date — so you always see the full movement,
not just the end result. Use the search box to find a payment by **name,
phone, email or service**.

<Note>
  Refunds you make directly in your Stripe dashboard show up here too, within
  moments. An external refund does **not** cancel the appointment — cancel it
  on your schedule as well if the service won't happen.
</Note>

## Fee transparency

If this store's plan has a Linkzee fee on booking payments, the exact amount and
percentage for the period is shown right under the numbers — and on the
**Scale** plan that fee is **0%**. See the fee table in
[Charge for bookings](/scheduling/payments#fees).

<Note>
  Earnings show the **active store**. The fee follows that store's plan, so if
  you run more than one store, switch stores to see the other one's numbers —
  see [Plan per store](/billing/plan-per-store).
</Note>

## Payouts — how the money reaches your bank

The **Payouts** section on the Earnings page explains the money flow and shows the
official numbers, straight from Stripe:

1. **Client pays** — the amount lands instantly in your Stripe balance.
2. **Transparent deductions** — Stripe deducts its processing fee, plus the
   Linkzee fee of this store's plan (when there is one).
3. **It hits your bank** — payouts are automatic, on the standard schedule for
   your country and payment method (PIX settles faster than cards).

Each payout in the list shows the **exact net amount** that reaches your bank,
with every deduction itemized — the same official statement you'd see in
Stripe.

<Note>
  The dashboard numbers are the gross amounts processed through bookings.
  Stripe's processing fees are deducted at payout — the payouts list is where
  you see the final net values.
</Note>
