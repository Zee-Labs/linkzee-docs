Social proof sells. After each appointment, Linkzee asks your client for a
quick review — and your **average rating with stars** appears at the top of
your booking page, where new visitors decide.

</Frame>

## How reviews are collected

About **2 hours after an appointment ends**, the client gets a review request —
by **WhatsApp** (when your WhatsApp channel is active) or by **email**. The
link opens a simple page: **1 to 5 stars** and an optional comment. No account,
no friction.

<Note>
  Only people who **actually booked** can review — each review link is tied to
  one appointment, and each appointment can be reviewed **once**. No fake
  reviews, no duplicates.
</Note>

## Where your rating shows

Once you have reviews, your booking page shows the **average and the count**
(for example ⭐ 4.9 · 23) right at the top — visitors see it before picking a
service.

## Managing reviews

In **Scheduling → Settings → Reviews** you see every review. You can **hide** a
review (it stops counting toward your public average) and show it again
anytime.

<Note>
  Reviews can be hidden, but never **edited or deleted** — that's what keeps
  your rating trustworthy for visitors.
</Note>
