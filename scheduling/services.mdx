Services are what your visitors book. Before anyone can pick a time, you set up
the list of services you offer — each with its own duration, price and style.
Add them in **Scheduling**, and everything you change saves automatically and
shows up in the live preview.

## Create a service

Add a service for each thing people can book — a haircut, a consultation, a
tattoo session. For each service you set:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What the service is called (for example "Gel manicure"). |
| **Duration** | How long the appointment lasts. |
| **Price** | Optional — show it or leave it off. |
| **Buffer** | Extra time reserved *after* the appointment, so you're never booked back-to-back. |
| **Color** | A color to tell your services apart at a glance. |
| **Description** | A short line explaining what's included. |
| **Online meeting** | Toggle on to create a **Google Meet** link automatically. |

Set the fields that matter for each service — only the **name** and
**duration** are essential, and the rest are up to you.

## Combos

A **combo** bundles 2 or more of your services into one bookable item — "Cut +
Beard", "Consultation + Return visit" — with its **own price and duration**
(often a better deal than booking separately). Create one in **Services** by
choosing the **Combo** type and picking which services it includes; your page
lists what's inside automatically.

Visitors can also build their own bundle at booking time: after picking a
service, they can **add another service** to the same visit — Linkzee sums the
durations and finds a slot that fits everything (with a team, only
professionals who do *all* the chosen services are offered).

## Charging for a service

Every service can charge online at booking — a **deposit** (percentage or
fixed) or the **full amount**. Set it per service under **Online charging**;
see [Charge for bookings](/scheduling/payments) for how to activate payments.

Services can also be sold upfront in [session packages](/scheduling/packages)
or included as benefits in [recurring plans](/scheduling/subscription-plans).

## Online meetings

Turn on **Online meeting** for any service that happens over video. When it's on,
Linkzee creates a **Google Meet** link for each booking automatically, so you
don't have to send one by hand.

<Note>
  When a service has **Online meeting** turned on, the visitor's **email becomes
  required** at booking — that's how the Meet link is delivered to them.
</Note>

To use online meetings you'll connect your Google Calendar first. See
[Google Calendar](/scheduling/google-calendar) for the setup.
