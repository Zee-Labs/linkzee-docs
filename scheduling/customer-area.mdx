Every Linkzee page with scheduling gets a **customer area**: a private space
where your customers check their upcoming appointments, package credits and
subscriptions, all by themselves. It works for bookings made on the Linkzee
agenda and on the [Qzee agenda](/scheduling/qzee-booking) of your bio.

Customers reach it from your bio or at `linkz.ee/yourusername/me`. Both take
them to the same page in a separate, isolated Linkzee area, so the address in
the browser changes to `vault.linkz.ee` (see [Privacy](#privacy)).

## My bookings on your bio

Anyone who already booked on a device sees, at the top of your bio:

- **Your next appointment**, a card with the day, the time and who will see
  them, when an appointment is coming up;
- **My bookings**, inside the booking card, which opens the customer area.

Nothing to set up: both appear on their own after the first booking on that
device, whether it was made on the Linkzee agenda or on the Qzee agenda.

## How customers sign in

**On the device they booked with**, there is no code and no password. The
customer area opens with the bookings made on that device.

**On another device**, they tap **Booked on another device?** and then **Get a
code**. The identity is the **phone number they use to book and buy**:

1. They type the phone number they use to book and buy.
2. Linkzee sends a **6-digit access code** to the e-mail on their record (and
   by WhatsApp, where available).
3. They enter the code and they're in. The session lasts 90 days and only works
   for your page.

<Note>
  The code is sent only if the phone number matches an existing customer of
  your page. Codes expire in 10 minutes, and resending is limited to protect
  you and your customers from abuse.
</Note>

The code brings back Linkzee agenda bookings, package credits and
subscriptions. Bookings made on the Qzee agenda show up on the device used to
book.

## What they can do

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **My appointments** | Upcoming and past bookings, with **Reschedule** and **Cancel**. |
| **My packages** | Remaining sessions per package and the expiry date. |
| **My subscriptions** | Active plan, benefits, usage in the current cycle and renewal date, plus **Manage billing**, which opens the secure Stripe portal to update the card or cancel. |

The list highlights the **next appointment** (date, "in 2 days", professional,
Reschedule and Cancel buttons), then the **Upcoming** ones in order and the
**Past** ones collapsed, each with **Book again**. Times show in each booking's
own time zone. For services served in order of arrival, the time reads "from
9:00 AM".

### Bookings on the Qzee agenda

Qzee bookings sit in the same list as the others:

- **Reschedule** opens your Qzee agenda in a panel, straight on the step to
  pick a new time.
- **Cancel** happens right in the customer area, after the customer
  confirms. The rule comes from your Qzee agenda: cancelling before the time
  you set there doesn't count as a late cancellation, and after it the customer
  can still cancel until the appointment time.
- The buttons follow what Qzee allows for each booking. When neither
  rescheduling nor cancelling is possible, the customer sees **Contact** with
  your business name.
- If the business changed the appointment after the list loaded, nothing is
  cancelled: the customer sees the new time and confirms again.
- **Book again** appears only while the unit takes online bookings.

Bookings made on the same device at another business's Qzee agenda appear
under **Other bookings on this device**, with that business's name.

## Privacy

What lets a customer see and change a booking stays on their own device, in a
separate and isolated Linkzee area (`vault.linkz.ee`). It never sits on your
bio page, so no script on the page, including your own tracking tags, can reach
it. Your bio only remembers that the device has booked with you and a short
reminder of the next appointment, so it can show **Your next appointment**.

<Note>
  Customers who signed in with a code on a device before this change confirm a
  code once more. No booking is lost.
</Note>

## Why it helps you

- Fewer "what time was my appointment?" messages: customers self-serve.
- Package and subscription buyers can always see what they've paid for, which
  builds trust in prepaid bundles.
- Linkzee agenda cancellations respect the **cancellation window** you set in
  Bookings → Settings, and covered bookings return their credits automatically.

<Note>
  Share the customer area with anyone: it's simply your page URL followed by
  `/me`. A good habit is to include it in your bio or send it after a
  purchase, so customers know where to find their credits and bookings.
</Note>
