Every Linkzee page with scheduling gets a **customer area** at
`linkz.ee/yourusername/me` — a private space where your customers check their
upcoming appointments, package credits and subscriptions, all by themselves.

## How customers sign in

No passwords. The customer's identity is the **phone number they use to book
and buy**:

1. They open `linkz.ee/yourusername/me` and type their WhatsApp number.
2. Linkzee sends a **6-digit access code** to the e-mail on their record (and
   by WhatsApp, where available).
3. They enter the code and they're in. The session is short-lived and only
   valid for your page — safe on shared devices.

<Note>
  The code is sent only if the phone number matches an existing customer of
  your page. Codes expire in 10 minutes, and resending is limited to protect
  you and your customers from abuse.
</Note>

## What they can do

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Appointments** | Upcoming and past bookings; they can **cancel** within your cancellation policy. |
| **Package credits** | Remaining sessions per package and the expiry date. |
| **Subscriptions** | Active plan, benefits, usage in the current cycle and renewal date — plus **Manage billing**, which opens the secure Stripe portal to update the card or cancel. |

## Why it helps you

- Fewer "what time was my appointment?" messages — customers self-serve.
- Package and subscription buyers can always see what they've paid for, which
  builds trust in prepaid bundles.
- Cancellations respect the **cancellation window** you set in Scheduling →
  Settings, and covered bookings return their credits automatically.

<Note>
  Share the customer area with anyone: it's simply your page URL followed by
  `/me`. A good habit is to include it in your bio or send it after a
  purchase, so customers know where to find their credits and bookings.
</Note>
