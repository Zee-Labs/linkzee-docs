Connect your **Google Calendar** so your appointments appear right on your phone,
next to everything else in your day. Once it's linked, Linkzee and Google stay in
sync both ways — new bookings flow into Google, and the times you're busy in Google
stop being offered on Linkzee.

## Connect your calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your scheduling settings">
    Go to **Scheduling → settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the Google card">
    Click **Connect** on the Google card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Sign in with your Google account and authorize Linkzee. You'll come back
    to the dashboard with a **"Google Calendar connected"** confirmation — and
    from that moment, bookings start appearing in your calendar automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens once you're connected

- **Every booking is added to your Google Calendar** automatically, so it shows up
  wherever you check Google — phone, laptop, or watch.
- **Services with the online meeting option get a Google Meet link** created for
  them automatically, ready to share with the visitor (see [Services](/scheduling/services)).

## Respect your Google events

Turn on **respect my Google events** to keep your two calendars from clashing.
When it's on, any time you're already busy in Google — a personal errand, another
meeting, a day off — is hidden from your Linkzee booking times. The sync works
both ways, so you never get double-booked.

<Note>
  You can disconnect anytime from the same screen. Your existing appointments stay
  on your Linkzee schedule.
</Note>
