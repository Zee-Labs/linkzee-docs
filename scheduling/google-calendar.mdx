Connect your **Google Calendar** so your bookings show up right on your phone, next to everything
else in your day. Linkzee creates its own calendar inside your Google Calendar —
**"Linkzee · {your business name}"** — and puts every booking there, with a phone reminder before
it starts.

## Connect your calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the scheduling settings">
    Go to **Scheduling → Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the Google card">
    Click **Connect** on the Google card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Sign in with your Google account and tick the permission **"Make secondary Google calendars,
    and see, create, change, and delete events on them"**. You land back on the dashboard with
    **"Google Calendar connected"**, and the "Linkzee" calendar already shows in Google.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Linkzee only touches the calendar it created. Your other events are never read, edited or
  deleted — to respect your busy times, Linkzee only sees "free" or "busy", with no titles or details.
</Note>

## What happens once you're connected

- **Every booking goes into the "Linkzee" calendar**, which appears in your Google calendar list
  (phone, computer or watch) with its own color. Show or hide it with one tap.
- **Phone reminder before each booking.** On the Google card, pick the timing: no reminder, 10,
  15 or 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, or 1 day before (default: 30 minutes). It applies to bookings
  synced after the change.
- **Services with online meetings get a Google Meet link** created automatically (see
  [Services](/scheduling/services)). Some work accounts have Meet turned off by the admin; in that
  case the card tells you, and video services won't get an automatic link.
- **Rescheduled or cancelled in Linkzee?** The event follows right away. Changes made directly in
  Google don't flow back to Linkzee — always reschedule in Linkzee.

## Respect your Google events

Turn on **Respect my Google events** to avoid conflicts. When it's on, any time you're busy in your
**main** Google calendar — a personal appointment, another meeting, a day off — stops being offered
on Linkzee.

<Note>
  For this to work, the box **"View your availability in your calendars"** must be ticked on
  Google's permission screen. It's optional: without it the connection works, but your events
  don't block times — the card tells you, and you just connect again ticking the box.
</Note>

## Does the card show "Connect again"?

Older connections used a different Google permission and put bookings in your main calendar. They
keep working, but the card shows **Connect again**: once you reconnect, upcoming bookings go to the
"Linkzee" calendar with phone reminders. Bookings already made stay where they are — with the same
Meet link.

<Note>
  Deleted the "Linkzee" calendar in Google by accident? It's recreated automatically on the next
  booking. You can disconnect any time from the same screen.
</Note>

## Google Calendar and linked calendars

Google events only block the business where the account was connected; they do **not** carry over to the person's other calendars on Linkzee. To make a time taken in one business busy in the other, use [Linked calendars](/scheduling/linked-calendars).
