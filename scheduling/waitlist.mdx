Fully booked is good news — unless a cancellation leaves a hole in your day.
With the waitlist, visitors who didn't find a time can ask to be notified, and
when a slot opens up, **the first compatible person in line gets it offered
automatically**.

## How visitors join

When someone opens your booking page and the day has no available times, they
see a **"tell me when a slot opens"** option: they pick the date range and
period (morning, afternoon, evening — or any time), leave their name and
WhatsApp, and they're in line.

## What happens when a slot opens

When a booking is cancelled:

1. Linkzee finds the **first person in line** whose service, professional and
   time window match the freed slot.
2. They get a **WhatsApp message** with a link to claim it — valid for
   **2 hours**.
3. One tap confirms the booking (if the service charges online, they complete
   the payment to confirm). If they don't respond in time, the offer moves to
   the **next person** in line automatically.

## Fair-use limits

- A person can have up to **3 active waitlist entries** per page.
- A waitlist entry covers a window of up to **60 days**.

## Managing the queue

In **Scheduling → Settings → Waitlist** you see everyone in line — name,
phone, requested window and status — and you can remove entries manually.
