Your page can include an **AI assistant** that handles scheduling by chat. Instead
of walking through the booking wizard, visitors just ask — and the assistant takes
care of the rest, politely and clearly.

## What the assistant can do

The AI assistant works with your live schedule, so it always stays in sync with
your services and availability.

- **Lists your services** so visitors know what they can book.
- **Lists your team** and lets the visitor pick a professional — if you run a team,
  the assistant can offer the choice; otherwise it just assigns whoever is free.
- Offers **real available times** only — never a slot you can't take.
- **Books** an appointment for the visitor.
- **Reschedules** an existing appointment to a new time.
- **Cancels** an appointment when the visitor asks.
- **Recognizes what the visitor already has booked.** When a returning visitor
  starts a scheduling conversation, the assistant sees the appointments they already
  have (on that device) and asks whether they want a **new** one, or to **reschedule**
  or **cancel** an existing one — instead of blindly booking another.

## How a booking works in chat

The assistant guides the conversation in plain language and confirms every action
before it happens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The visitor asks">
    The visitor tells the assistant what they want — for example, to book a service
    or move an existing appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The assistant offers real times">
    It suggests only times that are actually free on your schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The visitor confirms">
    Each action — book, reschedule or cancel — is confirmed with a **button**, so
    nothing happens by accident.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Online services

For services with the online meeting option turned on, the assistant asks for the
visitor's **email** and lets them know the **Google Meet** link will arrive by
email. If the visitor loses the link later, the assistant points them back to
their **booking's manage page** and the confirmation email — the link lives in
both.

The assistant also handles payment hiccups gracefully: when a **payment link
expires** before the visitor finishes paying, it explains what happened and
offers to book a fresh time right away — no dead end.

<Note>
  The AI assistant has its own setup, covered under **AI assistant** in the docs.
  Turn it on there, and it uses the same services and availability you already set
  in **Scheduling**.
</Note>
