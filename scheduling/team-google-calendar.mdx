In **Team** mode, every staff member can connect **their own** Google Calendar —
it's optional, and each person's calendar stays separate from yours.

Because your staff don't sign in to Linkzee, you generate a **connection link**
for them and share it. Once they connect, their busy times block only **their**
slots, and their events and **Google Meet** links go to **their** calendar.

## Connect a staff member's calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team member">
    Go to **Scheduling → Team** and open the person you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the connection link">
    Click **Connect Google** to create a personal connection link for that
    staff member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Send the link to them — for example on WhatsApp — so they can authorize
    Google on their own device. If you're already on their device, you can open
    it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They authorize Google">
    They open the link and sign in to their Google account to finish connecting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The connection link is valid for a **limited time**, so ask your staff member to
open it soon after you send it. If it expires, just generate a new one.

<Note>
  Each staff member's connection is independent. One person can be connected
  while another isn't — bookings still work for everyone either way.
</Note>

## What happens once they connect

- Their **busy times** in Google stop being offered for **their** slots only.
- Every booking with that staff member is added to **their** Google Calendar.
- For services with the **online meeting** option, the **Google Meet** link is
  created on **their** calendar.

<Warning>
  Share the connection link only with that staff member — it connects their
  personal Google account.
</Warning>
