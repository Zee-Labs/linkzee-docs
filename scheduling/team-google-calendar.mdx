In **Team** mode, each staff member can connect their **own** Google Calendar — it's optional, and
each person's calendar stays separate from yours.

You generate a **connection link** and share it with the staff member (or they connect from their
own dashboard, under **Settings → Integrations**). Once they connect, Linkzee creates the
**"Linkzee · {business name}"** calendar in **their** Google: their bookings and **Google Meet** links
go there, with a phone reminder before each one, and busy times in their main calendar block only
**their** slots.

## Connect a staff member's Google Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the staff member">
    Go to **Scheduling → Team** and open the person you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the connection link">
    Click **Connect** to create a personal connection link for that staff member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Send them the link — over WhatsApp, for example — so they can authorize Google on their own
    device. If you're already on their device, you can open it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They authorize Google">
    They open the link, sign in to their Google account and tick the permission **"Make secondary
    Google calendars, and see, create, change, and delete events on them"**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The connection link is valid for a **limited time**, so ask them to open it soon after you send it.
If it expires, just generate a new one.

<Note>
  Each staff member's connection is independent. When a staff member hasn't connected their own
  Google, their bookings (and Google Meet links) go to **your** "Linkzee" calendar — the store's.
  The phone reminder timing is the one you chose on the Google card.
</Note>

## What happens once they connect

- Every booking with that staff member goes into the **"Linkzee"** calendar in **their** Google.
- For services with **online meetings**, the **Google Meet** link is created in that calendar.
- Their **busy times** in their main calendar stop being offered only in **their** slots — Linkzee
  only sees "free" or "busy", with no details.
- If you **move a booking to another professional**, the event leaves the previous calendar and
  shows up in the new one.
- Removing someone's access **disconnects their Google Calendar** from your business at the same time.
- Connections made before November 2026 show a **Reconnect link** button: generate the link and ask
  them to connect again. Bookings already made stay where they are.

<Warning>
  On Google's permission screen, the box **"Make secondary Google calendars, and see, create, change,
  and delete events on them"** must be ticked. If it's left unticked, Linkzee refuses the connection
  and the status explains what to tick — just open the link again.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Share the connection link only with that staff member — they connect their personal Google account.
</Warning>

## What about the staff member's other calendars?

The staff member's Google only blocks their times in **this** business. If they also take bookings elsewhere on Linkzee, [link the calendars](/scheduling/linked-calendars): a booking made there is busy here, and vice versa, without going through Google.
