title: Holidays
icon: calendar-star
---

Linkzee knows the public holidays where your business is (country, state and,
for Brazilian cities, the city itself) and asks you **once, 10 days before** each
one: *are you open that day?* One tap answers. You never have to remember a
holiday again, and nobody books a day you meant to close.

## How it works

1. **Confirm where the business is.** Scheduling → Settings → Holidays shows
   the location taken from your account ("Goiânia, GO · Brazil"). Confirm it once
   (or change it). Each business has its own location.
2. **Ten days before a holiday** you get an e-mail and a push: *"Is Vikings open
   on Tiradentes?"* with two buttons, **We're open** and **Closed that day**. The
   button opens a light page with a single **Confirm**. The same line appears,
   quietly, at the top of the Today view, and you can dismiss it.
3. **Closed** blocks the day for the whole team with one block per day; nobody
   can book it. If clients are already booked, Linkzee shows you who they are
   first, e-mails them the reschedule link and **never cancels anything on its
   own**: the Today view keeps "2 clients to reschedule on 21/04" until you sort
   them out.
4. **No answer? Nothing changes.** The agenda stays open. If you did not answer
   in time, a single reminder arrives 3 days before.
5. **Your choice is remembered** for next year (the holiday is identified by its
   rule, so moving holidays like Carnival still match).

## The list

Settings → Holidays lists the next 60 days: date, name, weekday and "in 10 days",
and the origin of each entry (National, your state, your city, Optional holiday,
Added). Carnival and other optional holidays ask too; observances such as
Mother's Day only appear. Holidays falling on a day you are closed anyway are
shown muted and never asked.

- **Add a date** for a local holiday Linkzee does not know (city anniversary,
  patron saint): it repeats every year.
- **Not a holiday here** hides an entry for good (you can bring it back).
- **Close automatically on national holidays** for businesses that always
  close: the day is blocked 10 days before and you still get the e-mail to open
  an exception.
- **Holiday e-mails** can be switched off per business; the push and the line
  in the Today view stay.

## For your clients

A closed day simply appears disabled in the booking form, like any other block,
with no reason shown. If you are open, nothing changes for them.

<Note>
  Holiday data comes from the open-source [date-holidays](https://github.com/commenthol/date-holidays)
  library (CC BY-SA 3.0) and, for Brazilian states and cities, from
  [feriados.dev](https://feriados.dev/) (MIT). Laws change and local data can be
  incomplete: the list is a suggestion, and the decision is always yours.
</Note>
