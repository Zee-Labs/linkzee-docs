title: Holidays
icon: calendar-star
---

Linkzee knows the holidays where your business is (country, state and, for
Brazilian cities, the city itself) and asks **10 days ahead**: *will you be
working that day?* You answer in one tap: **We'll be working**, **Reduced
hours** or **We won't be working**. Nobody books on a day you don't work, or
outside the hours you chose.

## How it works

1. **Confirm where the business is.** Calendar → Settings → Holidays shows the
   location from your account. Confirm it once (or change it). Each business has
   its own location.
2. **Ten days before**, you get an email and a phone notification. With one
   business and one holiday, the email has the three answers as buttons. If you
   worked reduced hours last year, the main button is ready ("Work from 8:00 AM
   to 12:00 PM"). The same question shows at the top of the Today tab.
3. **Nearby holidays come together.** Holidays that start up to 9 days after the
   first one join the same question (Carnival and Ash Wednesday; Christmas, its
   eve and New Year). Instead of several emails, you get one with an **Answer**
   button.
4. **No answer, no change.** Your calendar stays as it is. A single reminder
   arrives 3 days before, with only the holidays still unanswered.
5. **Your choice is saved** for next year: the question comes prefilled with
   what you did.

## The three answers

- **Working**: the day stays normal.
- **Reduced**: choose *from* and *to* (for example, 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM).
  Nobody can book outside those hours. It applies to the whole team; each
  professional keeps their own hours. Shortcuts appear as suggestions: *Same as
  last year*, *Morning only*, *Afternoon only* and, on holiday eves, the official
  half day.
- **Not working**: the whole day is blocked.

For multi-day holidays (Carnival, for example) you can answer day by day: not
working on Monday and reduced hours on Tuesday.

**Booked clients are never cancelled.** Before you confirm, Linkzee shows who
falls outside the new hours and, if you keep the switch on, emails them a link
to reschedule. Each client gets that notice only once, even if you change the
hours later. The Today tab keeps "3 clients outside the reduced hours on 12/24"
until you sort it out.

## Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and Ash Wednesday

These are official **half days** in some countries (in Brazil, the eves are
holidays from 2:00 PM and Ash Wednesday until 2:00 PM). They ask too, already
suggesting reduced hours that fit your schedule. If you don't open that day,
nothing shows up. Automatic blocking of national holidays never applies to half
days.

## More than one business

If you have more than one business, you get **a single email** with a block per
business (how many days each would open and how many clients already booked).
The **Answer** button opens a page with a card per business, prefilled from last
year, and each business keeps its own answer. **Same answer for all** only fills
in; nothing changes until you tap **Confirm**. Each business only shows on the
days it would open and on its own city's holidays. Businesses in different time
zones get the notice at 9 AM in each zone.

## The list

Settings → Holidays shows the next 60 days: date, name, weekday and "in 10
days", plus where each line comes from (National, your state, your city,
Optional holiday, Added). Each line has **Working · Reduced · Not working**;
with reduced hours the line shows "From 8:00 to 12:00 · Change". Observances
such as Mother's Day only show. A holiday on a day you already don't open is
greyed out and never asks.

- **Add a date** for a local holiday Linkzee doesn't know: it repeats every year.
- **Not observed here** hides the holiday this year and in the following years.
- **Don't work on national holidays** if you never work on them: the day is
  blocked when the notice goes out (about 10 days ahead) and you can still change it.
- **Holiday notices by email** can be turned off per business, or with the link
  at the bottom of the email itself; the phone notice and the Today tab stay.

Under **Unavailable days**, reduced hours show as a single line ("only 8:00 to 12:00"). Deleting it sets the day back to "Working".

## For your clients

A day you don't work shows as disabled in the booking form, like any other
block, with no reason. With reduced hours only the slots inside the window show,
and the bio badge says "Open now · closes at 12 PM" that day.

<Note>
  Holiday data comes from the open-source [date-holidays](https://github.com/commenthol/date-holidays)
  library (CC BY-SA 3.0) and, for Brazilian states and cities, from
  [feriados.dev](https://feriados.dev/) (MIT). Laws change and local data can be
  incomplete: the list is a suggestion, and the decision is always yours.
</Note>
