With **recurring plans** your customers subscribe to your services and are
charged automatically every month (or year) — like *"Pet Health Plan:
R$99/month with 1 consultation included and 20% off grooming"*. Predictable
income for you, effortless renewals for them.

<Note>
  Recurring plans require **online payments** to be active (see
  [Charge for bookings](/scheduling/payments)). Subscriptions are charged by
  card and processed by Stripe; the money goes straight to your account.
</Note>

## Create a plan

1. Open **Bookings → Packages** and go to the **Recurring subscriptions** section.
2. Tap **New subscription** and set:
   - **Name and description** — what subscribers see.
   - **Price and interval** — monthly or yearly.
   - **Benefits** — for each service, choose one of:
     | Benefit | What the subscriber gets |
     | --- | --- |
     | **Unlimited** | Book the service as often as they want, no extra charge. |
     | **Credits per cycle** | A number of covered sessions per billing cycle (e.g. 2 per month). Unused credits don't roll over. |
     | **Discount** | A percentage off when paying for that service. |
3. Save. Active plans appear on your public page next to your packages.

## What your visitors see

Your page lists each plan with price, interval and benefits. To subscribe,
the visitor enters their **name and WhatsApp number** (e-mail optional), then
pays the first charge by card — the next charges renew automatically on the
same card.

After payment — and if they entered an e-mail — a confirmation arrives with
the plan's benefits, and the subscription shows up in their [customer area](/scheduling/customer-area).

## How benefits apply when booking

- When a subscriber books, Linkzee recognizes their **phone number** and
  applies the best benefit automatically: prepaid package credits are used
  first, then subscription benefits, then member discounts on whatever is
  left to pay.
- **Credits per cycle** count by the **appointment date's** billing cycle and
  reset each cycle — no rollover.
- If a covered booking is **cancelled**, the cycle credit returns
  automatically.
- **No-shows** consume the cycle credit by default. The same option that
  returns package credits on no-shows (**Bookings → Settings**) also returns
  subscription credits.

## Managing subscriptions

- **You**: the **Subscribers** list in Bookings → Packages shows each
  subscription and its status.
- **Your customer**: from their [customer area](/scheduling/customer-area)
  they can open the secure **billing portal** to update the card or cancel.
  A cancelled subscription keeps its benefits until the end of the period
  already paid.
- If a renewal charge fails, Stripe retries it; while the payment is overdue
  the benefits pause until it's settled.

## Earnings

Every subscription charge appears in **Earnings** with the Linkzee fee
applied. The rate is set by your plan **at the moment the customer
subscribes** and stays fixed for that subscription's renewals — new
subscriptions always use your current plan's rate (see
[Plans & pricing](/billing/plans)).
