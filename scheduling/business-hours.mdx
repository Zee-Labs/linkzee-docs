Your **business hours** tell Linkzee when you can take appointments. They shape the
times visitors see in the booking wizard, so only real, available slots are offered.

## Set your weekly hours

For each day of the week, add one or more time ranges — for example 9:00–12:00 and
14:00–18:00 — or mark the day as **closed**. Add as many ranges as you need to match
your real schedule, like a lunch break in the middle of the day.

<Note>
  Everything auto-saves as you edit, and your booking card updates in the live
  preview right away — there's no "Save" button.
</Note>

## Booking rules

Fine-tune how far ahead people can book and how your times are offered.

| Rule | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Time step** | The spacing between offered times — 15, 30 or 60 minutes. |
| **Minimum notice** | How far ahead a visitor must book (no last-second bookings). |
| **Maximum advance** | How far into the future a visitor can book. |
| **Cancellation window** | How late a visitor may still cancel a booking. |
| **Approval** | Bookings are confirmed **automatically**, or wait for your **manual** approval. |

## Staff hours

If you work with a team, each staff member can have their **own hours** that override
these business hours. Anyone without their own hours simply follows the ones you set
here.
