A **session package** is a bundle of services sold at a single price — like
*"5 haircuts for R$150, valid for 90 days"*. Your customer pays once, gets the
credits instantly, and every time they book one of the included services the
credit is used automatically. Great for cash flow and for bringing customers
back.

<Note>
  Packages require **online payments** to be active (see
  [Charge for bookings](/scheduling/payments)) — the purchase is paid online
  and the money goes straight to your Stripe account.
</Note>

## Create a package

1. Open **Bookings** in the dashboard and go to the **Packages** tab.
2. Tap **New package** and fill in:
   - **Name** — what the buyer sees (e.g. *"5-session pack"*).
   - **Description** — optional, shown on your page.
   - **Included sessions** — pick each service and how many sessions it includes. A
     package can mix services (e.g. 4 haircuts + 1 beard trim).
   - **Price** — one single price for the whole bundle.
   - **Validity after purchase** — how many days the credits last, counted from
     the payment confirmation.
3. Save. Active packages appear on your public page automatically.

## What your visitors see

Your page shows a **Session packages** card listing each package with its
price, included sessions and validity. You can drag the card's position (and
hide or show it) in **Links**, like any other block.

Buying takes under a minute:

1. The visitor taps **Buy package** and enters their **name and WhatsApp
   number** (e-mail is optional — it's where the receipt goes).
2. They pay on the secure checkout. The payment methods offered depend on
   your Stripe account — Brazilian accounts can offer **PIX** alongside cards.
3. The confirmation screen shows their credits — and if they entered an
   e-mail, a receipt arrives with everything included.

<Note>
  The **phone number is the key**: when the same customer books one of the
  included services later, Linkzee recognizes the number and uses a credit
  automatically — no codes, no vouchers.
</Note>

## How credits are used

- When a customer with credits books an included service, the booking is
  **covered by the package** — the payment step is skipped for that service.
- Credits are used **per session** and only for the services included in the
  package.
- If a covered booking is **cancelled**, the credit **returns automatically**
  to the package.
- **No-shows** consume the credit by default. If you prefer to return the
  credit on no-shows, enable that option in **Bookings → Settings**.
- Credits **expire** at the end of the validity period you set.

## Where your customer tracks their credits

Customers see their remaining credits, validity and purchase history in
**their customer area** — see [Customer area](/scheduling/customer-area).

## Earnings

Package sales appear in your **Earnings** page like any other payment, marked
as a package purchase, with the Linkzee fee of your plan applied — the same
rates as booking payments (see [Plans & pricing](/billing/plans)).
