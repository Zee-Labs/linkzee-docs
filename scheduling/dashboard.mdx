Scheduling opens on the **Today** tab — your day at a glance — and the **Panel**
tab gives you the numbers, plus a simple way to keep appointments up to date
after they happen.

## The Today view

The first thing you see when you open Scheduling:

- A **week strip** at the top — each day shows a dot when it has bookings; tap
  a day to jump to it, use the arrows to move between weeks.
- A **Next appointment** card: who's coming, in how long, with a one-tap
  **WhatsApp** button and quick actions.
- The **day timeline**: every appointment as a block at its time, colored by
  status (with a team, by **professional** — each one has their own colour and
  photo, and pending bookings get a dashed border), with a ✓ when it's paid — and
  a red line marking *now*. Overlapping bookings sit side by side. **Blocks** show
  as a hatched area: lunch, a day off, a holiday.
- Tap an appointment for its **details and actions** (confirm, done, no-show,
  reschedule, cancel, WhatsApp). Tap an **empty time** in the grid and the new
  booking opens **already at that time** — if the time has passed, you can still
  log a visit that already happened (see below).
- With a team, filter the timeline **by professional** — the filter is also the
  professional pre-selected when you book.
- **Block** closes a time right from here (the whole business, or one
  professional) without leaving the day.

Prefer the classic list? Use the **grid/list toggle** at the top — your choice
is remembered.

The Today view is **live**: a new booking from your link, a client
cancellation, a reschedule made on another device or a confirmed payment show
up on their own within seconds, with no page reload. The small dot next to the
date shows the state: green is live; amber, reconnecting; grey, no automatic
updates (the view then re-checks itself every minute). Each professional's
portal works the same way.

### Booking for a client yourself

When you create a booking, the client field **suggests your existing clients**
as you type — search by name, phone or email. Pick one and the booking is
linked to that client, so you never end up with duplicate records; type a new
name and Linkzee creates the new client for you.

The form goes **service → professional → when → who**. With a team, the professionals
appear as photo chips — only those who offer the chosen service can be picked, or leave
**Automatic** and Linkzee assigns the first one free. Someone walked in without an
appointment, or you need to log a visit outside business hours? Turn on **Book outside
hours**: minimum notice and business hours no longer apply, but a time that is already
booked or blocked stays unavailable — it never creates a conflict. If the time has already
**passed**, Linkzee never turns that on by itself: it tells you, and asks you to confirm
**Log an appointment that already happened?** — the client gets no confirmation for it.
Under the button you always see what's still missing (service, time, client).

## Marking an appointment done

In **Today**, tap the appointment (or the **⋯** menu in the list view) and choose:

- **Mark as done** — the visitor attended.
- **No-show** — the visitor didn't show up.

Changed your mind? You can switch an appointment between **done** and **no-show** at
any time, so the numbers always reflect reality.

## Automatic check-off

You don't have to mark every appointment by hand. When you use
[reminders](/scheduling/reminders) and a visitor **confirms** their appointment,
Linkzee **marks it as done automatically** a couple of hours after the time passes.

<Note>
  These automatic ones are **highlighted for you to review** — if someone confirmed
  but didn't actually show up, just switch it to **no-show** in one tap.
</Note>

If the visitor **didn't confirm** (or you don't use reminders), the appointment
isn't touched — it lands in **To review** so you can decide.
