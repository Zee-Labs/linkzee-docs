The **Panel** tab in Scheduling gives you a quick read on your bookings, and a simple
way to keep them up to date after they happen.

## The numbers

Pick a window — **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** — and you'll see:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Confirmed" icon="circle-check">
    Upcoming appointments that are still going to happen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="To review" icon="clock">
    Appointments whose time has already passed and still need a decision from you —
    mark them **done** or **no-show**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Done" icon="trophy">
    Appointments the visitor attended.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No-shows" icon="user-xmark">
    Appointments the visitor didn't attend.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attendance rate" icon="chart-line">
    Share of finished appointments that were attended (done vs. no-show).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Estimated revenue" icon="wallet">
    Based on the price of your confirmed and done appointments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Marking an appointment done

Open the **Agenda** tab, tap the **⋯** menu on any appointment and choose:

- **Mark as done** — the visitor attended.
- **No-show** — the visitor didn't show up.

Changed your mind? You can switch an appointment between **done** and **no-show** at
any time, so the numbers always reflect reality.

## Automatic check-off

You don't have to mark every appointment by hand. When you use
[reminders](/scheduling/reminders) and a visitor **confirms** their appointment,
Linkzee **marks it as done automatically** a couple of hours after the time passes.

<Note>
  These automatic ones are **highlighted for you to review** — if someone confirmed
  but didn't actually show up, just switch it to **no-show** in one tap.
</Note>

If the visitor **didn't confirm** (or you don't use reminders), the appointment
isn't touched — it lands in **To review** so you can decide.
