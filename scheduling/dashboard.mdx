Scheduling opens on the **Today** tab — your day at a glance — and the **Panel**
tab gives you the numbers, plus a simple way to keep appointments up to date
after they happen.

## The Today view

The first thing you see when you open Scheduling:

- A **week strip** at the top — each day shows a dot when it has bookings; tap
  a day to jump to it, use the arrows to move between weeks.
- A **Next appointment** card: who's coming, in how long, with a one-tap
  **WhatsApp** button and quick actions.
- The **day timeline**: every appointment as a block at its time, colored by
  status, with a ✓ when it's paid — and a red line marking *now*. Overlapping
  bookings sit side by side.
- Tap an appointment for its **details and actions** (confirm, done, no-show,
  reschedule, cancel, WhatsApp). Tap an **empty space** in the grid to create a
  booking at that day.
- With a team, filter the timeline **by professional**.

Prefer the classic list? Use the **grid/list toggle** at the top — your choice
is remembered.

## Marking an appointment done

In **Today**, tap the appointment (or the **⋯** menu in the list view) and choose:

- **Mark as done** — the visitor attended.
- **No-show** — the visitor didn't show up.

Changed your mind? You can switch an appointment between **done** and **no-show** at
any time, so the numbers always reflect reality.

## Automatic check-off

You don't have to mark every appointment by hand. When you use
[reminders](/scheduling/reminders) and a visitor **confirms** their appointment,
Linkzee **marks it as done automatically** a couple of hours after the time passes.

<Note>
  These automatic ones are **highlighted for you to review** — if someone confirmed
  but didn't actually show up, just switch it to **no-show** in one tap.
</Note>

If the visitor **didn't confirm** (or you don't use reminders), the appointment
isn't touched — it lands in **To review** so you can decide.
