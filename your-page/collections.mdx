Collections group your links into labeled sections on your page — for example
**My courses** or **Discount coupons**. Instead of one long list, your links sit
under clear headings that visitors can scan at a glance.

</Frame>

## Create a collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a collection">
    On the **Content** screen, create a new collection and give it a name that
    describes what's inside, like **My courses** or **Discount coupons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add links to it">
    Move your links into the collection so they appear together under its heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename it anytime">
    Change the collection's name whenever you like — the new label shows on your
    page right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    Drag collections and the links inside them to set the order visitors see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything auto-saves as you go, and the preview updates live — on the right on
desktop, below the editor on mobile.

<Note>
  Collections are for **links**. Your products don't go into collections — they
  live in the product showcase instead. See [Products](/products/overview).
</Note>
