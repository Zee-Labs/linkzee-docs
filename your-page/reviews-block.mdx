Anyone can write a testimonial about themselves. On Linkzee, every review comes
from an **appointment that actually happened** — which is why the block carries
the line *"verified reviews from real bookings"*. That sentence is the whole
point: your visitors know the difference.

## Before you start

The block shows reviews collected by your calendar, so you need
**[Scheduling](/scheduling/overview) active** and **at least one review**
received. If you have no reviews yet, the block simply doesn't appear on your
page — never an empty section.

## Add the block

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your content screen">
    Go to **Links** and click **+ Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Reviews">
    Pick **Reviews**. Set how you want it to look (you can change this later)
    and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it into place">
    The block appears as a card in your list. Drag it by the handle to any
    position — above your links, below the booking card, wherever it converts
    best.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The toggle on the card turns the block on and off without losing your settings,
and the pencil reopens the options.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Layout** | **Carousel** (default — visitors swipe through), **List** (one under the other) or **Spotlight** (a single review, large) |
| **How many** | 3, 5 or 10 reviews |
| **Minimum rating** | All, 4 stars and up, or 5 stars only |

<Tip>
  Carousel is the safest default: it shows several reviews without pushing your
  links far down the page.
</Tip>

## What visitors see

The block opens with your **average and the number of reviews**, then the
reviews themselves: stars, the comment, the client's name, and which service
was booked. It uses your page's theme — colors, corners and fonts follow the
design you already chose.

<Note>
  **Privacy comes first.** Visitors only ever see a **short name** — first name
  plus the initial of the last name, like "Maria S.". Phone numbers and emails
  never leave your dashboard.
</Note>

## Choosing which reviews appear

You curate from **Scheduling → [Reviews](/scheduling/reviews)**:

- **Featured** reviews come first in the block.
- **Hidden** reviews never appear — and don't count toward the average.
- Everything else is ordered from newest to oldest, respecting the minimum
  rating you set.

Reviews are never edited or deleted, so what your visitors read is exactly what
your clients wrote.

## A bonus in search results

When your page also has an **address** (the map card), Linkzee publishes your
rating in the format search engines understand — which makes your page eligible
to show **stars in Google results**. Nothing to configure: add the address, keep
collecting reviews.
