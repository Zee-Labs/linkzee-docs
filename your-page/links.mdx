Links are the heart of your Linkzee page. Add any URL you want to share — a blog
post, a coupon, a YouTube video, your store — and it appears on your page right
away.

## Add a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content screen">
    This is where you build your page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + Add">
    Use the full-width **+ Add** button, then choose **Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Type a **title** and paste the **URL**. That's it — the link appears on
    your page instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Add modal: Link, Product, Social or Support — everything starts here.">
  <img src="/images/your-page/add-item-modal-en.webp" alt="Linkzee screenshot" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  Everything auto-saves as you type — there's no **Save** button. Your live
  preview updates in real time, on the right on desktop and below the editor on
  mobile.
</Note>

## WhatsApp links

Want a link that opens a **WhatsApp conversation** with you? When adding a
link, choose the **WhatsApp** destination: enter your number and an optional
pre-filled message, and Linkzee builds the correct `wa.me` link for you — no
manual URL crafting. Works on product buttons too.

## Add media to a link

A link can be more than text — give it a visual card:

| Media | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Image** | A photo or thumbnail shown on the card; visitors can tap to see it full-screen. |
| **Video** | A short clip (up to **60 seconds / 40 MB**) that plays right on your page. |
| **Embed** | Paste a **YouTube or Vimeo** URL and the player is embedded on the card. |

Open any link and pick the media type in the link editor. For an embed, choose
the **Embed** option and paste the YouTube or Vimeo URL there.

## Before & After and Gallery blocks

Besides links with media, your page can show **photo blocks** — cards whose
content is the photos themselves, with no destination URL (a Premium feature):

| Block | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Before & After** | Two photos stacked with a **draggable edge**: the visitor slides it to compare the result. You can rename the "Before"/"After" labels and choose where the edge starts. |
| **Gallery** | **2 to 10 photos** the visitor swipes through, with dots and a counter. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + Add and choose Before & After or Gallery">
    Each block has its own editor. Photo blocks can't live inside a collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the photos">
    Before & After takes exactly two photos (before on the left, after on the
    right). For a gallery, add 2–10 photos — on a computer, drag the files
    straight from a folder onto the photo area — and drag the thumbnails to reorder.
    Need to adjust a photo? **Reframe** lets you drag and zoom it inside the
    frame, just like the profile photo — what you see is what gets published.
    Use photos with the same framing — **4:5 (portrait)** works best; photos
    with different ratios are cropped at the edges to fit the frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm you have permission">
    Photos of clients are personal data. Tick the box confirming the people
    photographed agreed to the publication — it's required once per block, and
    the date of your confirmation is stored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The preview inside the editor shows the real block before you save. The
    photos are uploaded when you click **Save**; a block that is still missing
    photos stays hidden from your page until it's complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Photo blocks are **Premium**. On the free plan you can build and preview
  them, but saving asks you to upgrade. An optional caption appears over the
  photos; the block name is internal (it helps you and Zee find it). Photo
  blocks don't take the animated highlight effect — the photos are the focus.
</Note>

## Animated highlight

Want one link to stand out? The link editor has an **animated highlight**
effect that draws the eye to that card on your public page — the same effect
is available on products. It's a **premium feature**: saving a link with the
effect requires **this store** to be on a paid plan — if the store you're
editing is on Free, the editor prompts you to upgrade (see
[Plans & pricing](/billing/plans) and
[Plan per store](/billing/plan-per-store)).

## Manage your links

Each link has a few controls so you can keep your page exactly how you want it.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Pencil | Edit the title inline, right where it sits. |
| On/off toggle | Show or hide the link without deleting it. |
| Click counter | See how many times visitors have tapped the link. |
| Drag handle | Reorder links by dragging them up or down. |
| Delete | Remove the link — you'll be asked to confirm first. |

Reordering happens live: drag a link and the preview follows along, then the new
order saves on its own.

## Group links into collections

When you have a lot of links, group them into **collections** to create labeled
sections on your page — like "My courses" or "Discount coupons". It keeps your
page tidy and easy to scan.
