Add your social networks so their icons appear on your Linkzee page. Visitors
tap an icon to jump straight to your profile — no login or authorization needed.

## Add a social network

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add menu">
    On the **Content** screen, click the full-width **+ Add** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Social network">
    Pick **Social network** from the list of item types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a network">
    Choose a network from the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your handle">
    Type your **@handle** or **URL**, then add it. That's it — your icon appears
    on your page right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There's no login or authorization step. You just paste your handle or link,
  and Linkzee builds the icon for you.
</Note>

## Supported networks

- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- TikTok
- YouTube
- X
- Facebook
- Threads
- Pinterest
- Website (a generic link for anything else)

## Turn a social network into a card

Besides the icon row, you can promote one of your networks to a **full card**
on the page — great for the channel you most want people to visit. The card
comes in two sizes: **wide** (takes the whole row) or **compact** (a square
that sits side by side with other items). The icon row stays as it is; the
card is an extra placement for that network.

## Choose where the icons appear

Set the **position** for your social icons:

- **Top** — above your content.
- **Bottom** — below your content.

Everything auto-saves and the preview updates live — on the right on desktop,
below the editor on mobile — so you can see exactly where your icons land as you
change the position.
