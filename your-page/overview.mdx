The **Content** screen is where you build your page — it's the home of your
Linkzee. From here you add everything your followers will see and arrange it
exactly how you want.

## Add your first item

At the top of the screen you'll find a full-width **+ Add** button. Tap it to add
one of three kinds of items:

## Other blocks you can add

Beyond links, products and social icons, the **+ Add** menu offers blocks that
appear as cards on your page. Each one can be dragged to any position, just
like a link:

| Block | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reviews** | Your verified reviews from real appointments — see [Reviews block](/your-page/reviews-block) |
| **Map** | Your business address, with a card that opens directions |
| **Review us on Google** | A button that sends visitors straight to your Google review page |
| **PIX support** | A tip card so fans can support you directly — see [Share and support](/your-page/share-and-support) |
| **Booking** | The card that opens your calendar, added when you turn on [Scheduling](/scheduling/overview) |

<Note>
  Blocks only appear when they have something to show. The Reviews block stays
  hidden until you have reviews; the map needs an address. Your page never
  shows an empty section.
</Note>

### The Map block

Add your **address** and the card shows a map with a **Get directions** button
that opens the visitor's maps app. You can pick the map's look — **light**,
**dark**, or **auto** to follow your page theme.

### Review us on Google

Paste your **Google review URL** (from your Google Business Profile) and the
card sends visitors straight to your review form — a simple way to turn happy
customers into public reviews. Clicks on the card are measured like any link.

## Manage every item

Each item on the Content screen gives you the same set of controls:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drag handle | Reorder the item within its group |
| Pencil | Edit the title inline |
| URL | The address the item points to |
| On/off toggle | Show or hide the item on your page |
| Click counter | See how many taps the item has received |
| Delete | Remove the item — you'll be asked to confirm |

## How items are grouped

Items stay organized in fixed groups so your page always looks tidy:

- **Products always stay grouped together** as your showcase, in a fixed position.
- **Links stay grouped together** in their own section.

Dragging reorders your **links** *within* their section — it never mixes
products with links. Your product showcase is ordered automatically, with your
newest products first.

<Note>
  Everything you edit auto-saves — there's no **Save** button. On desktop your
  page updates live in the phone preview on the right; on mobile the preview
  appears below the editor.
</Note>
