The **Header** is your page's identity — the first thing visitors see at the top
of your Linkzee. It's where you set how your name appears, add a short bio, and
upload your profile photo.

## Choose your title style

Pick how your title shows at the top of your page:

- **Text** — your name appears as styled text.
- **Logo** — upload your logo image and it shows in place of the text.

When you choose **Logo**, upload your logo file. When you choose **Text**, your
name is displayed as text instead.

## Add your name and bio

Enter your **name** and a short **bio** that tells visitors who you are or what
you share. The bio can be up to **160 characters**, and a counter shows how much
space you have left as you type.

<Note>
  Keep your bio short and clear — one line that sums you up works best on a
  mobile screen.
</Note>

## Upload a profile photo

Add a **profile photo** so your page feels personal and recognizable. It appears
alongside your name at the top of your page.

Everything you change here reflects in the live preview right away — on the right
on desktop, and below the editor on mobile. It all saves automatically, so
there's no Save button to remember.

## Hero video

With the **Hero** header layout you can show a short looping video at the top of
your page instead of your photo. Hero video is a **Premium** feature: on a free
plan you'll see the lock before choosing a file, and nothing is uploaded.

Upload an **MP4 or MOV** file up to **40 MB** — a clip from your phone or an
AI-generated video both work. Linkzee optimizes it for you automatically:

- resized to **720p** and trimmed to the **first 15 seconds**;
- **silent** — the audio track is always removed, so your page never plays sound;
- looped, and usually under **~3 MB**, so it starts instantly for visitors.

While it's processing you'll see **"Uploading… N%"** and then **"Optimizing…"**.
You can leave the screen — processing continues in the background and the video
is applied to your page as soon as it's ready.

The first frame becomes the video's **cover**: it's shown while the video loads,
and it's what visitors see when they prefer reduced motion or have data saver on.

### Managing your hero video

- Use the **Photo | Video** toggle to switch what the hero shows. Switching back
  to Photo keeps your video — nothing is deleted.
- Open the **⋯** menu for more options:
  - **Frame** — drag and zoom over the cover to choose the framing; the same
    framing is applied to the video.
  - **Replace** — upload a different video.
  - **Remove** — delete the video (you'll be asked to confirm).

Visitors get a small ⏯ control to pause or play the video. On devices that block
autoplay, a ▶ button appears instead so they can start it themselves.

<Note>
  If your plan goes back to Free, your hero video is hidden from your public page
  — not deleted. It comes back as soon as you upgrade again.
</Note>
