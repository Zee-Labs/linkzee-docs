Made for DJs, artists and anyone whose page should **sound** like them: paste a
Spotify link and your bio gets an embedded player. Turn on **Stage Mode** and
the page comes alive with the music.

## Spotify Card

Add a link as usual (**My Linkzee → Add → Link**) and paste a
`open.spotify.com` URL — a track, album, playlist or artist. Linkzee detects
it, pre-fills the **title and cover**, and the link becomes a **playable
card**: visitors press play and the music starts right on your page, in one
tap, without leaving your bio.

- Works with tracks, albums, playlists and artist profiles.
- The music **keeps playing while visitors scroll** your page.
- No Spotify login needed to listen to previews; listeners with Spotify get
  the full experience.

<Note>
  You can also add Spotify as a regular **social network icon** (your profile
  link) — the card is for when you want the music front and center.
</Note>

## Stage Mode

**Stage Mode** makes your page react to the music playing in your Spotify
Card. With the **Pista** theme — designed for DJs, with equalizer bars along
the bottom of the page — the bars **dance to the beat** while a track plays.

Turn it on in **Design → Motion → Stage Mode**.

<Note>
  Stage Mode is a **premium** feature. Visitors who prefer reduced motion (a
  system accessibility setting) always see the calm, static version.
</Note>
