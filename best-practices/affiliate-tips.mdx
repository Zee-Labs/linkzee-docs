Linkzee is built for **marketplace affiliates**, and a few habits make a big
difference in how much you sell. These tips help your offers look like a real
shop, feel urgent, and stay fresh — all while keeping your affiliate tracking
intact.

## Sell like a shop, not a list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the product showcase">
    Add your offers as products in your **product showcase** instead of plain
    links. Each one gets an image, a name and a real "buy" feel, so your page
    looks like a store — far more convincing than a list of raw URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show prices and promotions">
    Add a **price** and turn on a **promotion badge** so the value is obvious at
    a glance. When visitors see the deal clearly, they tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create urgency on limited deals">
    For time-limited offers, turn on the **scarcity counter**. A countdown
    nudges undecided visitors to act now instead of later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your offers fresh">
    Refresh your products regularly as offers change. Retire expired deals and
    add new ones so your showcase always reflects what's actually available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every change **auto-saves** and your live page updates in real time, so you can
swap offers on the fly — no Save button, no waiting.

## Let the link do the work

When a visitor taps a product, Linkzee redirects them to the marketplace and
keeps your **affiliate link** intact — so your commission is tracked. Just paste
your marketplace link into the product, and the tap does the rest.

<Note>
  Paste the affiliate link exactly as your marketplace gives it to you. Linkzee
  preserves it on redirect, so your tracking stays intact and you get credit for
  the sale.
</Note>

## Keep learning

- **Highlight your best deal** so it stands out at the top of your showcase.
- **Watch what sells** and lean into the products your audience taps most.
- **Share your link everywhere** — `linkz.ee/your-name` in your Instagram,
  TikTok and WhatsApp bios sends more people to your offers.
