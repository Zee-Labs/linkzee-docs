A great link in bio is easy to scan in seconds. When someone opens
`linkz.ee/your-name`, they should understand who you are and where to tap without
thinking. A little structure goes a long way.

## Start with a clear header

Your **header** is the first thing visitors see, so make it count. Add a photo or
logo and a one-line bio that says exactly what you offer. Keep it short — one
sentence that a first-time visitor understands right away.

<Note>
  Your header photo and name also power your link previews and search results, so a
  clean, current header represents you everywhere you share.
</Note>

## Put your most important items first

Visitors read from the top down, and many never scroll to the bottom. Lead with the
one link or offer that matters most today, then order the rest by importance. If you
are running a promotion, that belongs near the top.

## Group links into collections

When you have several links, tidy them into **collections** so each section is easy
to find. Collections turn a long list into clear groups — for example "This week's
deals", "My favorites" or "Contact me" — so visitors jump straight to what they
want.

## Keep it focused — fewer, better links

A short, sharp list beats a long one. Every extra link makes the important ones
harder to find, so remove anything that no longer earns its place. Fewer, better
links keep your page fast to scan and easy to act on.

Do the same for your **product showcase**: keep it focused on your current offers so
it reads like a real, up-to-date shop rather than an archive.

<Note>
  Every change auto-saves and your live preview updates as you edit — on the right on
  desktop, below the editor on mobile — so you can rearrange your page and see the
  result instantly.
</Note>
