More visitors is only half the story — the other half is getting each one to tap.
These are simple, proven ways to grow the clicks on your Linkzee page. Every change
you make saves automatically and updates your live preview right away, so you can
try each idea and see it instantly.

## Put your best offer first

People scan from the top down, and many decide within seconds. Lead with your most
important link or offer so it's the first thing visitors see. Drag it to the top of
your page and let everything else follow.

<Note>
  Your preview shows exactly what visitors see — check that your best item sits above
  the fold on the phone mockup.
</Note>

## Keep the list short and scannable

A long wall of links is easy to ignore. Keep your page short so each item stands out,
and remove anything that isn't pulling its weight. Fewer, stronger links almost always
beat a long list.

## Write clear, action-oriented titles

Tell people what they'll get when they tap. Action-oriented titles convert far better
than vague labels.

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Link | Shop the sale |
| My store | Browse today's deals |
| Click here | Get 20% off |

## Highlight your best product

Turn on **Highlight** for your top product so it appears larger and stands out in your
showcase. Use it on the offer you most want people to notice — one clear hero beats
five equals.

## Create urgency

A little urgency nudges undecided visitors to act now instead of later.

- Add a **promotion badge** (like "OFERTA") so a deal reads as a deal at a glance.
- Turn on the **scarcity counter** on limited offers so visitors see time counting down.

<Note>
  Use urgency where it's real — on genuine sales and limited offers. It works best when
  visitors can trust it.
</Note>

## Share your link everywhere

Your page can only earn clicks if people find it. Put your `linkz.ee/your-name` link
everywhere your audience already is:

- Your Instagram, TikTok and YouTube bios
- Your WhatsApp status and profile
- Your posts, stories and video captions

The more places your link lives, the more taps it earns.
