A few small settings make a big difference between an empty calendar and a busy
one. Use these tips to make booking easy for your visitors and reliable for you.

## Name services clearly with realistic durations

Give each service a plain, recognizable name and a duration that matches real
life. When the time you block is accurate, back-to-back bookings actually fit —
and your visitors know exactly what they're signing up for.

## Add a buffer between appointments

Turn on a **buffer** so you're not booked back-to-back. A short gap gives you
time to reset, run late without a domino effect, and start each appointment
calm instead of rushed.

## Turn on reminders

Enable **reminders** a few hours before the appointment. A timely nudge is the
single easiest way to cut no-shows, because most people simply forget — not
cancel.

## Connect Google Calendar

Connect **Google Calendar** so new bookings land on your phone automatically.
You see everything in one place, and your existing events keep those times from
being double-booked.

## Share your booking link everywhere

Put your **booking link** where people already find you: directly in your bio
and in your stories. The easier it is to reach, the more often it gets tapped.

<Note>
  Every change auto-saves and your live page updates in real time — set these up
  once and they're ready for your next visitor.
</Note>
