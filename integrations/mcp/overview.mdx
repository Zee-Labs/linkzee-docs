**MCP** (Model Context Protocol) lets AI agents — like **Claude**, **ChatGPT** or
**Cursor** — control your Linkzee in plain language. Just ask, and the agent does
the work for you:

- "Add this product to my page."
- "What do I have booked this week?"
- "Change my theme."

It's the friendliest way to manage your page hands‑free.

## How it works

The agent connects to Linkzee's MCP endpoint at `https://api.linkz.ee/mcp` using
your **API key**. Once it's connected, you talk to your agent normally and it
updates your Linkzee for you — no menus, no clicking.

<Note>
  Everything the agent changes saves automatically and updates your live page in
  real time, just like editing it yourself.
</Note>
