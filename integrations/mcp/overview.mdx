**MCP** (Model Context Protocol) lets AI agents such as **Claude**, **ChatGPT**
or **Cursor** control your Linkzee in plain language. Just ask, and the agent
does the work for you:

- "Build my bio from scratch with these services and photos."
- "Add this product to my page."
- "What do I have booked this week?"
- "Change my theme and put the reviews card above the map."

An agent connected to Linkzee can do everything you do in the editor for your
public page: read the real state, create links and media blocks, send photos and
videos, set the header and the design, configure the special cards and decide
the order of the page.

## How it works

The agent connects to Linkzee's MCP endpoint at `https://api.linkz.ee/mcp`.
There are two ways to sign in:

| Way | Best for | How access is decided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Claude connector** (OAuth) | claude.ai, the Claude desktop app and the Claude mobile app. No key to copy. | You sign in to Linkzee and choose the groups: `content` (required), `agenda`, `leads` and `finance` (optional). |
| **API key** (`lzk_`) | Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT, automations and any MCP client that sends an `Authorization` header. | The key scopes: `read`, `write` and the optional `finance`. |

Both act on **one store**: the store the key was created in, or the store you
picked when you connected Claude. Using the API and MCP needs a plan that
includes them (Pro or Scale).

<Note>
  Everything the agent changes saves automatically and updates your live page in
  real time, just like editing it yourself.
</Note>

## What an agent needs from you

- **Photos and videos.** The agent can use public URLs. When the files are only
  on your phone or in the chat, it creates an upload link and you pick the files
  on a Linkzee page. See [Sending files](/integrations/mcp/sending-files).
- **Consent for photos of people.** Before media blocks with photos or videos of
  people go live, the agent asks you to confirm that the people shown agreed.
- **Your agreement before an import.** Importing a Linktree or Beacons page
  replaces your profile and adds links, so the agent shows a preview first.
