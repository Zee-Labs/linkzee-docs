Once you connect an AI agent through MCP, it can manage almost every part of your
Linkzee for you — just by asking in plain language. This page lists what an agent
can do, grouped by area, so you know what to ask for.

<Note>
  Actions that **read** your data need a key with the **read** scope. Actions that
  **create, update or delete** need a key with the **write** scope. See
  [API keys](/integrations/api/api-keys) to set the right scope.
</Note>

## Page & profile

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Storefront | Read your whole page — your profile, links and products in one view. |
| Profile | Update your name, photo, bio and other profile details. |
| Appearance | Set your appearance and theme. |
| Map & socials | Update your map and your social networks. |

## Links & collections

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Links | Create, update and delete your links. |
| Collections | Group links into collections and manage them. |
| Order | Reorder your content so your best items come first. |

## Products & categories

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Products | Create products one at a time or in bulk. |
| Product edits | Update or delete existing products. |
| Categories | Create and manage your product categories. |

## Design

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Update your design and switch your theme. |

## Analytics & leads

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Analytics | Read a summary of how your page is doing. |
| Leads | List the leads you've captured. |

## Scheduling

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Agenda | Configure your scheduling setup. |
| Services & team | Manage your services, staff and blocks. |
| Appointments | List and create appointments, and check availability. |
| Changes | Reschedule appointments and change their status. |
| Google | Check the connection status of Google. |

## AI assistant

| Tool area | What the agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| Assistant | Manage your AI assistant's configuration. |

<Note>
  Everything an agent changes auto-saves and updates your live page in real time,
  exactly as if you'd made the change yourself in the editor.
</Note>
