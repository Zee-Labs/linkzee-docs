The **public API** lets you automate your Linkzee from your own tools or scripts.
Manage your **products**, **links**, **content**, your **agenda**, and your
**leads** programmatically — so Linkzee fits right into your workflow.

## What you can do

- **Sync a product feed** so your showcase stays in step with your offers.
- **Bulk-update offers** — prices, promotions and more, all at once.
- **Wire Linkzee into your workflow**, connecting it to the tools you already use.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point to the base URL">
    All requests go to `https://api.linkz.ee`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with an API key">
    Every request is authorized with your **API key**. Create one and keep it
    safe before you start.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Prefer natural language? You can also let AI agents control your Linkzee in
  plain words with **MCP** — see the [MCP overview](/integrations/mcp/overview).
</Note>
