Linkzee works right in your browser, but you can also **install it as an app** on your
phone or computer. You get a home-screen icon, a full-screen experience without the
browser bar, and — the big one — **push notifications** for new leads and bookings.

There's nothing to download from an app store: it installs straight from your browser.

## Install on Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Linkzee in Chrome">
    Sign in to your dashboard on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Install">
    Tap the **Install app** button when it appears, or open the browser menu (⋮) and
    choose **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm, and the Linkzee icon appears on your home screen like any other app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install on iPhone and iPad

Safari doesn't show an install button, so you add it from the Share menu:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Linkzee in Safari">
    Sign in to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Share">
    Tap the **Share** icon (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On iPhone and iPad, [push notifications](/account/notifications) only work **after**
  you install the app — so this step unlocks alerts for new leads and bookings.
</Note>

## What you get

- A **home-screen icon** and full-screen app, no browser bar.
- **Push notifications** the moment you get a new lead, a new booking — or a
  booking payment lands.
- Quick shortcuts to your page, earnings, performance and plan.
- Works offline for the parts already loaded, with a friendly screen when you lose
  connection.

## Your followers can install your page too

Your public Linkzee page is installable as well. When someone visits your page, they
can add it to their home screen — with **your name and your photo** as the app icon.
It's a great way for your biggest fans to keep you one tap away.
