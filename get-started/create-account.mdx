There are two ways to create your Linkzee account. Pick whichever is easiest for
you — both get you to the same place.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email and password">
    Enter your email and choose a password. After you sign up, Linkzee sends you
    an activation email. Click the link in it to activate your account. The link
    expires after a few hours — if it does, just request a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with Google">
    Click **Continue with Google** and pick your account. There's no password to
    set, and your account is active right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in

When you come back, sign in with the same method you used to sign up — your
**email and password**, or **Continue with Google**.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?** and
enter your email. Linkzee sends you a reset link so you can choose a new password.
The reset link is valid for a short time.

<Note>
  If an email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. The sender is Linkzee.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Activation and password reset links are single-use and time-limited. If a link
  has already been used or has expired, request a new one from the sign-in screen.
</Warning>
