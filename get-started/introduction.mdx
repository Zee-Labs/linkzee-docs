Linkzee is your **link in bio**: a single mobile‑first page at `linkz.ee/your-name`
that holds everything you want to share — your links, a **product showcase** for
your affiliate offers, your social networks, and even a **booking calendar**.

It's built for **marketplace affiliates** (Shopee, Amazon, Mercado Livre and more):
paste a product link and Linkzee fills in the name, image and price for you, so your
followers can buy in one tap while you keep your affiliate commission.

## Why creators choose Linkzee

- **Made for affiliates.** Paste a marketplace link and the product fills itself in.
- **One page, everything.** Links, shop, socials and bookings live together.
- **Always your style.** Curated themes keep your page looking great, no design skills needed.
- **Works everywhere.** Your page is fast, mobile‑first and ready to be found on Google.
- **Your language.** Linkzee speaks Portuguese, English, Spanish, French and German.

<Note>
  Everything you edit saves automatically and updates your live page in real time —
  there's no "Save" button to remember.
</Note>
