Two places in the dashboard menu, under **Resources**, help you learn Linkzee
at your own pace: **Start Here**, a checklist for your first steps, and
**Academyzee**, a library of short video lessons.

## Start Here

**Start Here** gets your page ready to sell in four steps. Linkzee checks off
what you've already done automatically, so you always see what's next.

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Your page is live** | Copy your link and paste it into your Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp bio. |
| **Profile: photo and name** | Use a close-up photo. It shows inside a circle, so the edges are cropped. |
| **Your products in the showcase** | Add at least one product. Write each category the same way every time, so the showcase filter and the assistant find it. |
| **Enable the AI Assistant** | Fill in the knowledge base (prices, timelines, policies) so the assistant knows what to answer. |

At the top, a progress bar shows how many steps are done and names the next
one. The screen also has:

- **Copy link** and **View my bio**, so you can share your page right away.
- A **video tutorial** with the complete walkthrough.
- **Common questions** with quick answers about WhatsApp links, the
  assistant, leads, the profile photo, colors and the Meta Pixel.

## Academyzee

**Academyzee** gathers 30 short video lessons in seven tracks:

| Track | What you learn |
| --- | --- |
| **First steps** | Build your page from scratch: name, links, look and video. |
| **Booking** | Let clients book on their own, pay a deposit and get a reminder. |
| **Anna, your AI assistant** | Turn her on, teach her and let her answer and close for you. |
| **Zee, your copilot** | Ask by text or voice, and Zee does it in your dashboard. |
| **A bio that sells** | Showcase, map and blocks that turn visitors into clients. |
| **Grow with data** | See who visits, who clicks and where each client comes from. |
| **Advanced** | Connect Claude and build your whole page with AI. |

Each lesson card shows its length and, when the feature needs one, the plan
it belongs to. When a lesson ends, the next one in the track is suggested.

<Note>
  The lessons are recorded in Portuguese, with captions on screen.
</Note>

## The "How it works" button

Most dashboard screens show a **How it works** button next to the title, with
the length of the lesson (for example, **How it works · 3 min**). Tap it to
watch the lesson for that screen without leaving what you're doing.
