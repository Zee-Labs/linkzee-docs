Your Linkzee page lives at a public address built from your username:
`linkz.ee/your-name`. This is the link you'll share in your Instagram, TikTok
or WhatsApp bio, so it's worth choosing well.

## Username rules

Your username has to follow a few rules so your link works everywhere:

- At least **3 characters** long.
- **Lowercase** only.
- Letters, numbers and hyphens — **no spaces and no accents**.
- **Unique** across Linkzee — if someone already has it, you'll need another one.

## Tips for a good username

- Keep it **short and memorable** so people can type it from memory.
- Match your **@ on social media** when you can, so your audience recognizes you
  instantly across every platform.

## When you choose it

You pick your username during **onboarding**, right after you create your account.
You can **change it later** from your account settings — but changes are meant to
be rare and deliberate, because your address is what's printed in your bios and
QR codes:

- After a change you have a **24-hour window** to adjust it freely (the "typo" case).
- Then the address is **locked for 14 days** before the next change.
- **Going back** to an address your store already had is always allowed.
- An address you release is **reserved for 90 days** — no other account can take it
  during that time.

<Note>
  Changing your username changes your public link everywhere — the old
  `linkz.ee/your-name` will stop opening your page. Remember to update it in all
  your bios.
</Note>
