Already have a page on **Linktree** or **Beacons**? You don't have to rebuild
it by hand — Linkzee imports it for you: links, photo, bio, social networks
and products.

## Where to find it

- **During onboarding** — when you create your account, choose
  **Bring my page over** instead of starting from scratch.
- **Any time later** — on the **Links** screen, open **+ Add** and choose the
  import option.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste your page link">
    Enter the URL of your current page (for example
    `linktr.ee/yourname`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Linkzee reads the page and shows everything it found — your profile
    photo, bio, links, social networks and products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to bring">
    Everything is selected by default; untick anything you'd rather leave
    behind, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The imported items land on your page like any other content — reorder, edit
or delete them freely, and the live preview updates as you go.

<Note>
  The import copies your **content**, not your old design. Pick one of
  Linkzee's themes in **Design** to give the page its new look — see
  [Themes](/design/themes).
</Note>
