Categories keep your product showcase organized. When you sell a lot of items,
grouping them into **categories** makes your page easier to scan — and lets your
visitors browse straight to what interests them.

## How categories work

Create a category, assign products to it, and arrange the order. On your public
page, visitors can browse your products by category instead of scrolling through
one long list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a category">
    Add a new category and give it a clear name, like "Kitchen" or "Best sellers".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign products">
    Add products to the category so they show up together in that group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    Drag your categories to set the order they appear in your showcase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything auto-saves as you go, and the preview updates live — on the right on
desktop, or below the editor on mobile.

<Note>
  Categories are the right tool when your showcase grows. If you only have a few
  products, you can skip them and let your products sit in one simple group.
</Note>
