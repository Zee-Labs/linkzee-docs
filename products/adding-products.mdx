Adding a product turns an affiliate link into a card in your showcase. Paste the
link and Linkzee reads the marketplace page to fill in the details, so you rarely
have to type anything.

## Add a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add menu">
    On your **Content** screen, click the full‑width **+ Add** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Product">
    Pick **Product** from the list of item types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the product link">
    Paste the **product link** from the marketplace (Shopee, Amazon, Mercado Livre
    and more). Linkzee reads the page and **auto‑fills** the name, image and price
    when it can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and adjust">
    Check the fields and edit anything that didn't fill in automatically. Your
    product appears in the showcase right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Product fields

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required. |
| **Description** | Optional, keep it short. |
| **Image** | Required — auto‑filled from the link, or upload your own. |
| **Product link** | The marketplace URL your button sends visitors to. |
| **Price** | Optional, in R$. |

<Note>
  If auto‑fill can't read a store, just fill in the **Name** and upload an
  **Image** yourself — that's all a product needs.
</Note>

Everything you edit auto‑saves, and the live preview updates as you go — on the
right on desktop, or below the editor on mobile.
