The scarcity counter adds a sense of urgency to your products. A countdown sits
on the product card, showing your visitors that the offer won't last forever —
a simple nudge that gets more people to tap **buy**.

## How it works

You set a **relative time** — how long is "left" on the offer, like
"30 minutes left", "2 hours left" or "3 days left". You never pick a fixed
calendar date.

Because the time is relative, your visitors always see a **countdown ticking
down**. It never shows as expired, and it never lands on "0", so your offer
always feels live and current — no matter when someone opens your page.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    Open the product you want to promote in your showcase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the scarcity counter">
    Enable the counter and choose the amount of time left — in **minutes**,
    **hours** or **days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The countdown appears on the product card right away. Your changes save
    automatically and the live preview updates as you edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Because the counter is relative, it restarts appropriately for each visitor —
  so the countdown always looks fresh, and no one ever arrives to an expired
  offer.
</Note>

Use the scarcity counter for limited promotions and flash offers you want to
push to the top of your visitors' minds.
