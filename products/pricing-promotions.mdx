Make your products stand out in the showcase. With a price, a promotion badge and
the highlight option, you turn a plain affiliate link into an offer people want to
tap. Every change you make here saves automatically and updates the live preview
right away.

## Set a price

**Price** is optional. Show it when it helps the sale, or leave it off when you'd
rather not.

- Enter the price in **R$** to display it on the product card.
- Leave it blank and the card simply shows no price.

<Note>
  If you added a product from a marketplace link, the price may already be filled
  in for you. You can always adjust it or clear it.
</Note>

## Add a promotion badge

The **promotion badge** puts a short label on the product card to signal a deal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the badge">
    Open the product and toggle **Promotion badge** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a short text">
    Set a brief label like `OFFER` or `50% OFF`. Keep it short so it fits the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The badge appears on the card in the live preview as soon as you set it.

## Highlight your best offer

Turn on **Highlight** to make a product appear larger and at the top of your
showcase, so it's the first thing visitors see.

Use highlight for your best offer — the deal you most want people to notice.
