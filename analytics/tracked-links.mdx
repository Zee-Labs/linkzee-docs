When all your visits come through the same link, you can't tell whether your
clients found you on Instagram, on WhatsApp or on the sign at your counter.
Linkzee solves this with **one short link for each place** you share your page.
They all open your normal page — nothing changes for your visitors — and
[Analytics](/analytics/overview) shows where each visit came from.

## Share your page

Tap **Share** (in the side menu, or at the top of the screen on your phone) to
open the share sheet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick where you'll paste it">
    Under **Where will you paste it?**, pick the network or place: Instagram,
    TikTok, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook, **Printed QR**, **Booking** or
    **Somewhere else**. **More networks** adds Threads, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest and
    E-mail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the spot on that network">
    When a network has more than one spot, choose it — for Instagram: **Bio**,
    **Stories** or **Direct**. A tip tells you exactly where to paste the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy">
    Tap **Copy link** (or **Send** on your phone to share it straight to an app).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each link is your address plus a short code — for example
`linkz.ee/your-name/ig` for your Instagram bio and `linkz.ee/your-name/igs` for
Stories. These links are **free on every plan**.

| Place | Spots |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | Bio · Stories · Direct |
| TikTok | Bio |
| WhatsApp | Message · Status |
| YouTube | Channel · Description |
| Facebook, Threads, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest | Profile |
| E-mail | Signature |
| Printed QR | Print · Social media |

- **Booking** gives you a link that opens your **scheduling directly**, marked
  with where the client came from (Instagram, WhatsApp, an ad or somewhere else).
- **Somewhere else** is your usual address, `linkz.ee/your-name`. Visits through
  it show up as **Direct** or as the site they came from.
- **Link for Google Analytics and ads** (under the short link) is the same
  destination with the UTM parameters written out in full, for ad platforms and
  tools that need them.

<Note>
  Visits that come through a short link appear in **Analytics → Where they came
  from** under that network and spot — for example **Instagram · Stories**.
  Numbers refresh about once an hour.
</Note>

## Campaign links

A campaign link is a link of your own for **a promotion, a partnership or a
printed card**. You choose its name and ending — for example
`linkz.ee/your-name/blackfriday` — and Analytics shows the visits, % who clicked
and bookings (or contacts) it brought in.

| Plan | Active campaign links |
| --- | --- |
| Free | — |
| Essential | Up to 10 |
| Pro and Scale | Unlimited |

To create one, open the share sheet and tap **Create campaign link**:

1. Enter the **Campaign name**. It also appears in the address for anyone who
   opens the link.
2. Under **Where you will use it**, pick the network and spot.
3. Check the **Link ending** Linkzee suggests, or type your own (2 to 24
   lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens).
4. Optionally, open **Customize UTM parameters** (advanced).
5. Tap **Create and copy**. You can also download the link's QR code right away.

You can also create and manage campaign links in **Analytics → Where they came
from → Campaign links**, where each one has copy, QR code, archive and a detail
view.

<Note>
  Campaign links are never deleted, only **archived** — so anything you've
  printed never starts pointing somewhere else, and an ending is never reused.
  An archived link keeps opening your page, and unarchiving it counts against your
  plan's limit again. To change a link's parameters, create a new one.
  If you change plans, links you've already shared keep working.
</Note>

## QR code and sign

From the share sheet, tap the **QR code** button at the top, or pick **Printed
QR** and then **QR code and sign**. Choose how you'll use it:

| Use | Formats | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **For print** | **A5 sign** · **Square** (sticker) | **PDF for printing** with 3 mm bleed, or **PNG** at 300 dpi to print at home |
| **For social media** | **Story** · **Post** | **PNG**, ready to post |

- **Style**: **Light** or **Dark**. The sign is black and white, so it prints well
  anywhere; the code itself is always dark on a white card, which phone cameras
  read best.
- **Title**: pick a ready-made title — for example *"Book your appointment"* (if
  your page has scheduling), *"See our services"* or *"Talk to us"*. The sign
  is written in your page's language.
- The sign shows your logo (or your initials), your address and *"Made with
  Linkzee"*.

Visits from the printed code show up in Analytics as **Printed QR**, and from the
social media version as **Printed QR · Social media**.

<Warning>
  The QR code carries your address. If you change your username, plan to reprint:
  when you've already downloaded a QR code or sign, Linkzee warns you on the
  username screen before you change it. See [Your username](/get-started/your-username).
</Warning>

## Privacy

Tracked links use Linkzee's own anonymous measurement, with no third-party
cookies. Where a visit came from is only used for your store's statistics and is
never shared with anyone.
