The **themes gallery** is where you pick your look. Each theme is a
**complete, ready-made palette** — background, buttons, fonts and text colors
that already match — so a single tap restyles your whole page at once.

## Pick a theme

Tap a theme to apply it instantly. The live preview updates right away (on the
right on desktop, below the editor on mobile), and your choice auto-saves —
there's no **Save** button to remember.

You can switch themes anytime. Picking a new theme replaces the previous look,
so it's easy to try a few and settle on the one that feels right.

## Fine-tune it yourself

At the end of the gallery you'll find a **Custom** option. Use it to adjust the
details — header layout, wallpaper, buttons, fonts and colors — beyond what the
theme sets for you.

<Note>
  Picking a ready-made theme again resets any custom tweaks back to that
  theme's look.
</Note>
