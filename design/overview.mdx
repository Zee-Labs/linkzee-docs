**Design** is where you choose how your page looks. Open **Design** from your
Linkzee editor to pick a theme and fine‑tune the details. Every change auto‑saves
and shows instantly in the live preview.

## Ready-made themes

Start from a **gallery of ready-made themes**. Each theme is a complete, harmonious
look — background, buttons, fonts and text colors that already work well together —
so your page looks great with a single tap.

## Curated fine-tuning

Want to make it yours? Beyond the theme, you can adjust a few curated areas:

| Area | What you control |
|---|---|
| **Header** | The layout of your name, photo or logo at the top |
| **Wallpaper** | The page background style |
| **Buttons** | Button shape and style |
| **Text** | The font, from a curated list |
| **Colors** | Accent and text colors, from a curated palette |

<Note>
  Choices are curated on purpose — you pick from options that always look good
  together, so there are no clashing colors or hard‑to‑read fonts to worry about.
</Note>
