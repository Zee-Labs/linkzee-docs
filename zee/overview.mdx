**Zee** is your personal AI copilot inside Linkzee. Instead of clicking through
menus, you simply **tell her what you want** — by voice or by text — and she gets
it done: creating products, reading your numbers, booking appointments, capturing
leads and giving you advice on how to grow. She's like having an assistant who
already knows your whole account.

<Frame caption="Zee full screen: ask about appointments, leads and your account's health.">
  <img src="/images/zee/zee-idle-en.webp" alt="Linkzee screenshot" />
</Frame>

Zee works alongside you, the owner. She always answers in **your account
language**, and for anything that reaches a client she **asks you to confirm first**.

<Note>
  Zee is available on the **Scale** plan, and the plan belongs to each store. On
  other plans you'll see her in the app with a prompt to upgrade — she opens once
  the store you're working in is on Scale.
</Note>

## What Zee can do

- **Insights & growth advice** — ask how your store is doing and what to improve.
- **Products** — create new products and edit the ones you have, just by describing them.
- **Metrics** — read your clicks, views and other numbers out loud or on screen.
- **Appointments** — book, reschedule or cancel appointments for you.
- **Agenda blocks** — block off time so no one can book when you're away.
- **Leads** — register a new contact, even from a photo or PDF you send her.
- **Client messages** — notify a client on your behalf, always after you confirm.

## Two ways to talk to her

- **Voice mode** — hold the microphone, speak, and Zee **answers out loud**. Great when your hands are busy.
- **Text mode** — type like a normal chat when you'd rather read the reply.

You can switch between the two at any time in the same conversation.

## Send her a photo or a file

Zee can read **attachments**. Send a photo of a handwritten client form or a PDF,
and she reads it and can, for example, **register the lead** from it for you — no
retyping.
