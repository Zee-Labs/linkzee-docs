Zee runs your store from a conversation. Open her, tell her what you need — by
voice or by text — and she takes care of it. She always replies in your **account
language**, and confirms with you before sending anything to a client.

## Opening Zee

Open Zee from your Linkzee dashboard. She starts a fresh conversation ready to
help — no setup needed.

<Note>
  Zee is a **Scale** plan feature. On the Free and other plans you'll see Zee with
  a prompt to upgrade; she opens once your account is on Scale.
</Note>

## Voice mode

Zee is built to be used hands-free.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold the microphone">
    Press and hold the **microphone** (push-to-talk) and say what you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let go to send">
    Release the button and Zee gets your request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="She answers out loud">
    Zee **speaks her reply** back to you, so you can keep working while you listen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Text mode

Prefer to read? Just **type** your message like any chat and Zee replies in text.
Use text when you're in a quiet place or want to keep the reply on screen.

## Sending a photo or PDF

Zee can read files you attach to the conversation.

- Snap a photo of a **client's paper form** and send it — Zee reads it and can
  **register the lead** for you.
- Attach a **PDF** and ask her about what's inside.

This turns a photo into an action, so you don't have to retype anything.

## What you can ask her

- **Get advice** — "How is my store doing this week? What should I improve?"
- **Read your numbers** — "Which product got the most clicks this month?"
- **Create a product** — "Add a product called Summer Sandals for R$89 with this photo."
- **Edit a product** — "Change the price of the blue backpack to R$120."
- **Book an appointment** — "Book Maria for a haircut tomorrow at 3pm."
- **Reschedule or cancel** — "Move João's Friday appointment to Monday at 10am."
- **Block your agenda** — "Block my agenda next Friday afternoon."
- **Register a lead** — "Save this contact: Ana, WhatsApp 11 99999-0000."
- **Message a client** — "Tell Maria her appointment is confirmed." Zee shows you
  the message and **asks you to confirm before it's sent**.

## Example prompts

<CodeGroup>
```text Insights
How did my page perform in the last 7 days, and what's the one thing I should fix?
```

```text Create a product
Create a product: "Wireless Earbuds", price R$149, mark it as featured, and use the photo I'm sending.
```

```text Scheduling
Reschedule Carlos's consultation from Thursday to next Tuesday at 2pm.
```

```text Lead from a photo
Here's a photo of a client form — register the lead with the name and phone on it.
```
</CodeGroup>

<Note>
  For anything that reaches a client — like a confirmation message — Zee always
  shows you what she'll send and **waits for your OK first**.
</Note>
