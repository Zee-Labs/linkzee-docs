Have a question about Linkzee? Start here. If you don't find your answer, we're
happy to help at `support@linkz.ee`.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Linkzee?">
    Linkzee is a **link in bio** built for marketplace affiliates. It gives you a
    single public page that combines your links, a **product showcase** for your
    affiliate offers, and a **booking calendar** — all in one place.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a website?">
    No. Your Linkzee page **is** your public page, live at `linkz.ee/your-name`.
    Share that link anywhere and everything you set up shows up there — no separate
    website needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do affiliate links work here?">
    You paste your marketplace link into a product. When a visitor taps that product,
    they're redirected to the store with your **affiliate link intact** — so your
    commission is preserved on every tap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my username later?">
    Yes. You can change it anytime in your **account** settings. Just remember to
    update the `linkz.ee/your-name` link wherever you've shared it — your Instagram,
    TikTok and other bios — so it keeps pointing to your page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages does Linkzee support?">
    Linkzee speaks **Portuguese**, **English**, **Spanish**, **French** and
    **German**. You choose your language in your account settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do my changes need saving?">
    No. Everything **auto-saves** as you edit — there's no Save button to remember.
    Your live page updates in real time, and the preview reflects each change
    instantly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can people book appointments from my page?">
    Yes. With **Scheduling**, visitors can book your services straight from your bio,
    including **Google Calendar** and **Meet** integration for confirmations and
    online meetings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can an AI assistant help my visitors?">
    Yes. You can add an **AI assistant** to your page that answers questions,
    recommends your products, and captures leads for you to follow up — in a friendly,
    on-brand way.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I automate my page?">
    Yes. Linkzee offers a **public API** and **MCP** support, so you can manage your
    page from your own tools or let AI agents control it in plain language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get help?">
    Reach out to us at `support@linkz.ee` and we'll be glad to help.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
