Something not behaving the way you expect? Most issues have a simple cause and an
even simpler fix. Find your symptom below and follow the steps.

Remember that everything in your editor **auto-saves** — there's no **Save**
button — and your **preview updates live** as you edit. If the preview looks
right but your public page doesn't, the section on caching below is usually the
answer.

## My changes aren't showing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cause">
    Your live page may still be showing a **cached** (older) version to visitors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix">
    Your edits **auto-save** as you make them, so nothing is lost. Wait a moment,
    then refresh your public page at `linkz.ee/your-name` to see the latest
    version.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## My product image won't upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cause">
    The file is likely **too large**, or it's in a format that isn't supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix">
    Use a common image format (like JPG or PNG) and a **smaller file**. Resizing
    the image before uploading usually does the trick.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto-fill didn't read my product link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cause">
    That store isn't supported for **auto-fill**, so Linkzee couldn't read the
    product details from the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix">
    Enter the product **name** and **upload an image** yourself. The link still
    works and keeps your affiliate tracking — only the automatic fill is skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## My social icons aren't showing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cause">
    The network is **toggled off**, or its **handle** is empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix">
    Open the social network and check that the **handle** or URL is filled in and
    that the item is **toggled on**. Your preview will update the moment it's set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Still stuck? Reach out to `support@linkz.ee` and we'll help you sort it out.
</Note>
