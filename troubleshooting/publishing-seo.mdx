Your Linkzee page is public at `linkz.ee/your-name` and everything you edit
auto-saves and updates live. If your page isn't showing up on Google yet, or a
shared link looks wrong, work through the sections below.

## My page doesn't appear on Google yet

**Symptom:** you search for your page on Google and it isn't there.

**Cause:** search engines take time to discover and index new pages. This is
normal for a page that's new or recently changed.

**Fix:** make sure your page is public and visit `linkz.ee/your-name` to confirm
it loads. Then share your link — on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and anywhere else
you're active — so it gets discovered. The more your link is out there, the sooner
it gets indexed.

<Note>
  Indexing can take days or weeks. There's nothing to submit — keep your page
  public and keep sharing your link.
</Note>

## My title or description didn't update

**Symptom:** the title or description shown in search results or link previews is
old or blank.

**Fix:** set your public page's **title** and **meta description** in your SEO
settings. Your changes auto-save, but search engines and apps often keep a cached
copy of the old preview for a while — give it time to refresh.

## Sharing shows the wrong image or name

**Symptom:** when you paste your link into a chat or social app, the preview shows
the wrong photo or name.

**Fix:** your preview image and name come from your profile — update your **header**
photo and name and they'll represent you everywhere you share. Some apps cache link
previews, so an old image may linger for a while after you update it.

<Note>
  Still stuck after everything looks right on your end? Reach out at
  `support@linkz.ee`.
</Note>
