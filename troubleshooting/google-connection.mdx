Scheduling works best when Google Calendar and Meet are connected. If something
isn't linking up, find your symptom below and follow the fix.

## Google Calendar won't connect

**Symptom:** you start the connection but it never finishes.

**Cause:** the Google authorization window was closed or interrupted before you
granted access.

**Fix:** complete the authorization in the Google window — sign in and accept the
requested permissions. Then try again from your **Scheduling** settings.

## My Google events aren't blocking times

**Symptom:** visitors can book you at times when you're already busy in Google
Calendar.

**Cause:** Linkzee isn't set to treat your Google events as busy time.

**Fix:** turn on **respect my Google events** in your **Scheduling** settings. Your
existing Google appointments will then block those slots from being booked.

## A staff connection link doesn't work

**Symptom:** a team member opens the link you sent to connect their calendar and it
fails.

**Cause:** the link expired.

**Fix:** generate a new staff connection link and share it again. Ask them to open
it promptly, before it expires.

## No Meet link on my appointment

**Symptom:** a confirmed booking has no Google Meet link for the online meeting.

**Cause:** the service didn't have the online meeting option turned on when the
booking was made.

**Fix:** enable the online meeting option on the **service**. New bookings for that
service will then include a Meet link.
