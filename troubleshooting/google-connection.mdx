Scheduling works best when Google Calendar and Meet are connected. If something
isn't linking up, find your symptom below and follow the fix.

## Google Calendar won't connect

**Symptom:** you start the connection but it never finishes, or you land back on the dashboard
with a connection error.

**Cause:** the Google window was closed before you clicked **Continue**, or the connection finished
in a different browser (for example, Google opened in another app) or with a different Linkzee
account logged in.

**Fix:** click **Connect** again and finish in the same browser. Google asks for a single
permission, with no box to tick: sign in and click **Continue**.

## My Google events aren't blocking times

**Symptom:** visitors can book you at times when you're already busy in Google
Calendar.

**Cause:** **Respect my Google events** is off, or Google hasn't granted the availability check
yet. That permission is asked separately, only when you turn the option on.

**Fix:** turn on **Respect my Google events** in your **Scheduling** settings and allow the
availability check on the Google screen. If the card shows a warning, click **Allow in Google**.
Declining keeps your connection working; only your Google events stop blocking times.

## A staff connection link doesn't work

**Symptom:** a team member opens the link you sent to connect their calendar and it
fails.

**Cause:** the link expired, or they finished the connection in a different browser from the one
where they opened the link.

**Fix:** ask them to open the link again and finish in the same browser. If it has expired,
generate a new staff connection link and share it again.

## No Meet link on my appointment

**Symptom:** a confirmed booking has no Google Meet link for the online meeting.

**Cause:** the service didn't have the online meeting option turned on when the
booking was made.

**Fix:** enable the online meeting option on the **service**. New bookings for that
service will then include a Meet link.
