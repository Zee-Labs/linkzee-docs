Can't get into your account? Most access problems come down to activation,
choosing the right sign-in method, or a password that needs resetting. Find your
symptom below and follow the fix.

## I didn't get my activation email

**Cause:** the email landed in spam, or the activation link expired before you
opened it.

**Fix:** check your **spam** and **promotions** folders first. The sender is
**Linkzee**, so search for that name. If the link has expired, request a new one
from the sign-in screen — activation links are time-limited, and a fresh link
always works.

## I can't sign in

**Cause:** your account isn't activated yet, or you're using a different method
than the one you signed up with.

**Fix:** confirm you activated your account from the email you received. Then use
the **right method** — if you created your account with email and password, sign
in that way; if you used Google, use **Continue with Google**. Still stuck? Reset
your password and try again.

## I forgot my password

**Cause:** you don't remember the password on your account.

**Fix:** open the sign-in screen and use the **reset link**. Follow the email to
choose a new password.

<Warning>
  The reset link is single-use and expires after a short time. If it's already
  been used or too much time has passed, request a new one.
</Warning>

## I signed up with Google

**Cause:** Google accounts don't have a separate Linkzee password.

**Fix:** always use **Continue with Google** to sign in. There's nothing to
reset — Google handles your login.

<Note>
  Still locked out after trying these steps? Email us at `support@linkz.ee` and
  we'll help you get back in.
</Note>
