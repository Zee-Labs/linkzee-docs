The **Linkzee Partner Program** pays creators and influencers a **30%
recurring commission** on the subscriptions they refer: as long as the people
you brought keep paying and you remain an active partner, you keep earning —
every single month.

## How to join

You need a Linkzee account (any plan — including Free).

1. Open **Partners** in your dashboard menu.
2. Fill in the application:
   - your **partner code** — it becomes your referral link and your coupon;
   - your **social channels** — pick the network, complete your handle;
   - your **audience** and **how you plan to promote** Linkzee.
3. Accept the [program terms](https://linkz.ee/p/partner-terms) and submit.

Applications are **reviewed manually** — we look at your channels to make
sure the program is a good fit. You'll get an e-mail when you're approved,
and the same page becomes your partner dashboard.

## Two ways to refer

| Tool | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Referral link** | `linkz.ee/?ref=yourcode` — anyone who signs up after clicking it counts as your referral (the attribution lasts 90 days, first click wins). |
| **Coupon** | Your code doubles as a checkout coupon giving a discount on the **first payment** (20% by default). Even without clicking your link, a subscriber who uses your coupon counts as yours. |

## What you earn

- **30% of every payment** made by your referred subscribers — recurring, not
  just the first month.
- Your partner dashboard shows clicks, sign-ups, active subscribers and your
  commission balance in real time.
- Commissions become **payable ~30 days** after the payment (the refund
  window). If a payment is refunded or disputed, its commission is reversed.
- **Payouts are monthly**, once your balance reaches **US$ 10**, paid via
  Stripe.

<Note>
  Commission duration follows the program's activity rule — active partners
  (bringing new subscribers regularly) earn for the **lifetime** of their
  referrals. The full rule, and everything else about the program, is in the
  [Partner Program Terms](https://linkz.ee/p/partner-terms).
</Note>

## Good to know

- Referrals are attributed to the **first** partner link the person clicked —
  coupons never override an existing referral.
- Self-referrals and paid-search abuse aren't allowed (see the terms).
- Tax documentation may be required before your first payout depending on
  your country.
