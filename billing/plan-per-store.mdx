title: Plan per store
icon: receipt
description: "Each store carries its own plan, its own subscription and its own invoice — subscribing one store doesn't unlock the others."
---

If your account has more than one store, the plan is **not** an account-wide
setting. Each store carries **its own plan**, with its own subscription, its own
invoices and its own renewal date.

Put simply: **subscribing store A does not put store B on a paid plan.** A store
you don't subscribe stays on **Free**, with Free's limits, no matter what your
other stores are paying for.

<Note>
  Only running one store? Nothing changes for you — your store is your plan, and
  the rest of this page is just there for the day you add a second one.
</Note>

## Subscribe a specific store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to the store first">
    Open the store switcher at the top of the sidebar and pick the store you
    want to put on a paid plan. Its name should be the one showing in the
    sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Plan">
    Go to **Dashboard → Plan**. Everything on that screen — your current plan,
    your usage, the upgrade options — is about the **active** store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the plan and check out">
    Pick the plan and the billing cycle, and finish the checkout. Payment is
    processed by Stripe, exactly as described in
    [Plans & pricing](/billing/plans).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Check the store name in the sidebar before you subscribe, upgrade, downgrade
  or cancel. Each of these actions applies **only** to the store that's active
  at that moment.
</Warning>

## What that means in practice

| | How it works with several stores |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | One per store. Each store you want on a paid plan needs its own subscription. |
| **Invoices** | Separate. Each store has its own payment history, its own invoices and its own PDF receipts, under **Dashboard → Plan** of that store. |
| **Billing cycle** | Independent. Store A can renew monthly on the 3rd while store B renews yearly in November. |
| **Upgrade & downgrade** | Applies to the active store. Proration, and the credit from a downgrade, land on **that store's** next invoice. |
| **Cancelling** | Returns **that store** to Free at the end of the period you already paid for. Your other stores keep their plans. |
| **Price you signed up at** | Tied to each subscription. A store that keeps its subscription active keeps the price it signed up at, independently of the others. |
| **Features and limits** | Read from the plan of the store you're in — products, analytics history, AI conversations, scheduling, WhatsApp reminders, Zee, public API and lead webhook. |
| **Linkzee fee on booking payments** | Follows the plan of the store where the booking happened: 4% on Free, 2% on Essential, 1% on Pro, 0% on Scale. |

<Note>
  Because the fee is read from the store's plan, a booking taken on a Free store
  keeps 4% even if another store in your account is on Scale.
</Note>

## The free trial is one per person

The **14-day free trial** belongs to **you**, not to a store. It's granted on
your **first** store, and it can only be used once — a store you create later
starts on **Free with no trial**, and deleting a store doesn't give the trial
back.

## Account or store?

| Belongs to your account | Belongs to each store |
| --- | --- |
| Your email, password and two-factor | Plan and subscription |
| Language, region and appearance | Invoices and billing cycle |
| The one-time 14-day free trial | Booking fee and feature limits |
| The list of stores you can switch between | Page, schedule, team and API keys |
