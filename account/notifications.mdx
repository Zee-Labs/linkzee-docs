Push notifications let Linkzee ping you the moment something happens on your page —
a new lead, a new booking — so you never miss what matters, even when the dashboard
is closed.

<Frame caption="Notification preferences: choose what reaches you by email and push.">
  <img src="/images/account/settings-notifications-en.webp" alt="Linkzee screenshot" />
</Frame>

They're opt-in: you turn them on, choose which kinds you want, and set quiet hours so
you're never woken up at night.

## What you can be notified about

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Leads" icon="bullseye">
    Someone new left their contact on your page or with your AI assistant.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Appointments" icon="calendar">
    A new booking, a reschedule, or a cancellation. When a booking payment is
    confirmed you get **"💰 You received \{amount\}"** instead of the new-booking
    alert — tapping it opens your Earnings page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Traffic" icon="chart-line">
    Milestones and spikes when your page is getting lots of visits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Account" icon="credit-card">
    Plan limits reached and payment issues to fix.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Turn on notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications settings">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the main switch">
    Flip the switch at the top. Your browser will ask for permission to send
    notifications — choose **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    Tap **Send test** to confirm a notification lands on your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The main switch is **per device**. Turn it on for each phone or computer where you
  want to receive alerts.
</Note>

## Choose which notifications you get

Under **Categories**, switch each type on or off — **leads**, **appointments**,
**traffic**, and **account**. These preferences apply across all your devices.

## Quiet hours

Don't want alerts overnight? Turn on **Quiet hours** and set a start and end time
(for example, 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM). During that window, notifications are held back so
your phone stays quiet.

## On iPhone and iPad

On iOS, push notifications only work once you've **installed Linkzee as an app** on
your home screen. If you don't see the option to turn them on, install the app first.

<Card title="Install the app" icon="mobile" href="/get-started/install-app">
  Add Linkzee to your home screen to unlock notifications on iPhone and iPad.
</Card>

<Tip>
  Prefer email? For important events like new leads, Linkzee also sends an email — so
  you're covered even when a device is offline.
</Tip>
