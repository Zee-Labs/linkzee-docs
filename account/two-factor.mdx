Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of security to your account. On
top of your password, you enter a **6-digit code** from an authenticator app on your
phone — so even if someone learns your password, they can't get in without your phone.

<Frame caption="The Security tab: enable two-factor authentication and manage sessions.">
  <img src="/images/account/settings-security-en.webp" alt="Linkzee screenshot" />
</Frame>

It's completely **optional** and off by default. You turn it on yourself, and you can
turn it off anytime.

## Turn on two-factor authentication

You'll need a free authenticator app such as **Google Authenticator**, **Authy** or
**1Password**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Go to **Settings → Security** and, under **Two-step verification**, tap **Turn on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app and scan the QR code on screen. Can't scan? Copy the
    setup key shown below the code and paste it into the app manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the 6-digit code">
    Your app now shows a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds. Type the current
    code to confirm the setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    Linkzee shows **10 backup codes**. Copy or download them and keep them somewhere
    safe — this is the only time they're shown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Save your backup codes before you finish. They're your way back in if you ever lose
  your phone, and they're shown only once.
</Warning>

## Signing in with 2FA

Once 2FA is on, signing in takes two quick steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Sign in as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your code">
    On the next screen, type the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Backup codes

Backup codes are single-use codes that work in place of your authenticator app. Each
one works **once**, then it's used up.

- Use one anytime the app asks for a code — tap **Use a backup code** and enter it.
- Running low? Go to **Settings → Security** and choose **Generate new backup codes**.
  Generating a fresh set **replaces** all your old codes.

## Lost your phone?

Don't panic — you have two ways back in:

- **Use a backup code** on the code screen when you sign in.
- Once you're back in, go to **Settings → Security**, turn 2FA off, and set it up
  again on your new phone.

<Note>
  If you've lost both your phone and your backup codes, contact
  [support](mailto:support@linkz.ee) to recover your account.
</Note>

## Signed up with Google?

If you sign in with **Google** and you've turned on 2FA, Linkzee still asks for your
6-digit code after Google — so your account stays protected either way.

## Turn off two-factor authentication

Go to **Settings → Security** and tap **Turn off**. For your safety, you'll need to
enter a valid code (from your app or a backup code) to confirm.
