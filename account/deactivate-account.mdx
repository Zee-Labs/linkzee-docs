Need a break? You can **deactivate** your Linkzee account. Deactivating pauses
your presence without deleting anything — when you're ready, sign in again and
everything is exactly as you left it.

## What happens when you deactivate

- Your public page goes **offline** — visitors to `linkz.ee/your-name` won't see
  it while your account is deactivated.
- Nothing is deleted. Your links, products, design and settings all stay in place.
- You can bring everything back at any time by **signing in again**.

<Warning>
  While your account is deactivated, anyone who visits `linkz.ee/your-name` won't
  see your page. Keep this in mind if you share your link in your bios or stories.
</Warning>

## Reactivate your account

To come back, simply **sign in** with your usual method. Your public page returns
online and your page looks just as it did before.

<Note>
  Deactivating is reversible and keeps all your content. If you'd rather remove
  your account permanently, contact `support@linkz.ee`.
</Note>
