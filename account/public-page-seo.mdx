Your Linkzee page is public and built to be found. A few settings control how it
looks in **Google search results** and in the **link previews** that appear when
you share your page on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else.

## Set your title and description

Open your **SEO** settings and fill in two fields:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Title">
    The headline for your page. It's what people see as the clickable link in
    search results and the bold line in a shared preview. Keep it short and
    describe who you are or what you offer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meta description">
    A short summary shown under the title. Use it to tell people what they'll
    find on your page and give them a reason to tap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both fields **auto-save** as you type, and your **preview updates live** — on the
right on desktop, and below the editor on mobile.

## Your preview image and name

You don't set a preview image separately. Linkzee pulls your **image** and
**name** from your profile automatically, so your **header photo** and name
represent you everywhere you share.

<Note>
  To change how your page looks when shared, update your header photo and name.
  Your search and preview details follow your profile.
</Note>

## Tips for a strong listing

- Lead with a clear, honest title — what someone would search for.
- Write a description that reads like an invitation, not a keyword list.
- Make sure your header photo looks good small, since it becomes your share image.

<Note>
  Search engines take time to index new pages. Keep your page public and share
  your `linkz.ee/your-name` link so it gets discovered faster.
</Note>
