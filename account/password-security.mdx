Keeping your account secure takes just a moment. Linkzee gives you a simple way
to change your password, recover it when you forget, and sign in with Google
without a separate password.

## Change your password

You can update your password anytime from your **account** settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account settings">
    Go to the **account** section from your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Enter your current password, then choose a new one and confirm it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Forgot your password?

If you can't remember your password, you don't need to be signed in to fix it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the reset link">
    On the sign-in screen, tap the reset link and enter your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the email">
    Open the email Linkzee sends you and follow the link to set a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Reset links are single-use and expire after a short time. If your link no
  longer works, request a new one from the sign-in screen.
</Warning>

## Signed up with Google?

If you created your account with **Google**, you simply sign in with Google —
there's no separate password to remember or change. Just choose **Continue with
Google** every time you sign in.
