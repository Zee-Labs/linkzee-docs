Your account and your store are **not** the same thing. You sign in once, and
inside that account you can run **several stores**. Each store is a complete,
independent Linkzee: its own public link, its own page content, its own
schedule and team, and its own **plan**.

This is how you keep two businesses apart, run a second brand, or set up a
separate page for a different city — without a second login and a second
password.

## Where your stores live

The **store switcher** is the first row of the left sidebar, right under the
Linkzee logo. It shows the store you're working on: its picture, its name, and
its public address (`@your-address`). A small arrow at the end of the row opens
the list.

On the phone there's no sidebar, so the same switcher sits at the **top of the
screen**, next to the menu.

Opening it shows every store you can work in:

- Stores you own, listed under **My store**.
- Stores where someone else invited you to work, listed as **You work as
  \{name\}**. If your access was ended, the entry keeps an **Ended** badge.

<Note>
  If you only have one store and can't create another, the row is just your
  store's name — there's nothing to switch to yet.
</Note>

## Create another store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the store switcher">
    Click your store's name at the top of the sidebar (or at the top of the
    screen on mobile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Create new store">
    At the bottom of the list, click **Create new store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Type the **store name** — that's the only thing you need — and click
    **Create store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Linkzee switches you to the new store and opens the same setup steps you
    went through the first time, so you can fill in your page from scratch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new store's **public address is created from the name** automatically. You
can change it later in **Settings → Public address**, exactly like your first
store.

<Note>
  You can have up to **5 stores** in one account. Try to create a sixth and
  Linkzee tells you the store limit for the account was reached.
</Note>

## Every new store starts on Free

A store you create is **on the Free plan**, and it does **not** get the 14-day
free trial. The trial is **one per person**, not one per store: it's granted on
your **first** store, and creating (or deleting and recreating) stores never
brings it back.

To put a new store on a paid plan, switch to it and subscribe — see
[Plan per store](/billing/plan-per-store).

## Switching stores switches everything

There's no confirmation and no reload: you pick a store and stay on the screen
you were already on, now showing that store's data. Anything you were editing
is saved to the store you were in before the switch.

From that moment on, the **active store** is what the whole dashboard is about:

| What | Belongs to |
| --- | --- |
| Plan, subscription, invoices and the Linkzee booking fee | The active store |
| Page content — links, products, design, header | The active store |
| Schedule, services, business hours and bookings | The active store |
| Team members and their invitations | The active store |
| Earnings, analytics and leads | The active store |
| API keys | The active store |
| Your login, password, two-factor and language preferences | Your account |

<Warning>
  Check the store name in the sidebar before you make changes, subscribe or
  cancel — every one of those actions lands on the **active** store.
</Warning>

## Team stays per store

Team members are invited into **one store**. Someone who works with you in
store A doesn't get store B, and their access is managed from store A's
scheduling settings. If you work in someone else's store, it shows up in your
switcher as a store you work in, not as a store you own — see
[Team](/scheduling/team).
