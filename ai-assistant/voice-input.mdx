title: Voice input
icon: microphone
description: "Let visitors speak to your AI assistant instead of typing — Linkzee turns their voice into text."
---

Not everyone wants to type. In your assistant's chat, visitors can tap the
**microphone** and simply **speak** their question. Linkzee turns their voice
into text and sends it to the assistant, which answers just as it would for a
typed message.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A visitor opens the chat">
    They tap your AI assistant on your public page to start a conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They tap the microphone">
    Instead of typing, they tap the **microphone** and speak their question out loud.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Linkzee turns speech into text">
    Their words become a text message and go straight to the assistant, which replies right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why it helps

- **Great on mobile.** Talking is faster than tapping on a small keyboard.
- **Perfect for longer questions.** Visitors can explain what they need in their own words without stopping to type.
- **Effortless for everyone.** It gives people a natural, hands‑free way to reach your assistant.

<Note>
  Voice input is part of the chat experience — there's nothing extra for your
  visitors to set up. They just tap the **microphone** and speak.
</Note>
