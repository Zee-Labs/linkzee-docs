The **AI assistant** is a smart helper you can add to your page. Visitors tap it
to open a chat, and it answers their questions, **recommends your products**, and
helps them decide what to buy — in a friendly, on-brand way, any time of day.

<Frame caption="Assistant setup: name, opening message and tone of voice.">
  <img src="/images/ai-assistant/ai-assistant-en.webp" alt="Linkzee screenshot" />
</Frame>

It can do more than talk. The assistant can **capture a visitor's contact** so you
can follow up later, and it can **handle scheduling** for you, turning a casual
question into a booking. It works around the clock, so no visitor goes unanswered.

## What it does

- **Answers questions** about your business, day or night.
- **Recommends your products** and helps visitors decide.
- **Captures contacts** so you can follow up on interested visitors.
- **Handles scheduling** so people can book without leaving the chat.

## Setting it up

You set up the assistant in your editor, under **AI assistant**. From there you
give it a voice, tell it about your business, and choose what it should collect
from visitors. As with everything in Linkzee, your changes **save automatically**
and the **preview updates live** — on the right on desktop, and below the editor
on mobile.

<Note>
  The assistant only talks about what you tell it about your business. The more
  you share about what you sell, your hours and your policies, the better it
  answers — and it never makes things up.
</Note>
