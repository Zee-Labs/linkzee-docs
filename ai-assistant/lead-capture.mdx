Every chat with your AI assistant is a chance to win a customer. With **lead
capture**, the assistant collects a visitor's details during the conversation and
saves them for you to follow up later.

## Turn on lead capture

In your editor, open **AI assistant** and enable **lead capture**. As with
everything in Linkzee, your changes save automatically and the live preview
updates as you go.

## Choose what to collect

Pick which fields the assistant asks for, and mark each one as required or
optional:

| Field | What it collects |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The visitor's name |
| **WhatsApp** | A WhatsApp number to reach them |
| **Email** | An email address |
| **Instagram @** | Their Instagram handle |

<Note>
  Ask only for what you truly need. A shorter form is easier to complete, so you
  capture more leads.
</Note>

## Choose when to ask

Decide the moment the assistant collects details:

<Steps>
  <Step title="At the start">
    Show a quick form before the chat begins. Choose this when you want a contact
    for every conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="At the end">
    Let the assistant ask naturally when there's a good moment in the
    conversation. Choose this for a friendlier, less demanding experience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find your leads

Captured contacts appear on your **Leads** page. From there you can review each
one to follow up, and export your list to work with it elsewhere.
