Your AI assistant answers from what you give it. The **knowledge base** lets you
feed it documents — so instead of typing every detail into its facts, you can
upload the files you already have and let the assistant use them in its replies.

## What you can upload

Upload **PDFs** and the assistant will draw on them when it answers visitors.
Good documents to add include:

- **Catalogs** — your full product range with details
- **Price lists** — up-to-date prices and options
- **Policies** — shipping, returns, warranty and terms
- **FAQs** — the questions your customers ask most

The more your documents cover, the better the assistant can help, recommend and
close the sale.

## Add a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your AI assistant">
    In your editor, go to **AI assistant** and open the **Knowledge base**
    section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a PDF">
    Add your file. Each file has a **size limit**, so keep documents reasonably
    sized — split a very large catalog into smaller PDFs if you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Linkzee do the rest">
    After you upload, Linkzee updates the assistant's knowledge **automatically**
    — there's nothing to save or publish. Your changes take effect on their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep your documents current. The assistant answers from what you upload, so
  when prices or policies change, replace the old file with an updated one.
</Note>
