Your AI assistant is only as good as what you tell it. On the **persona** setup
you give it a voice, a personality, and the facts it needs to help your visitors.
Open your editor, go to **AI assistant**, and fill in the details below. Everything
**auto-saves** as you type, and your preview updates live — on the right on
desktop, below the editor on mobile.

## What you set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name and title">
    Give your assistant a **name** and a **title** — for example a friendly first
    name and a role like "Shopping helper". This is how it introduces itself in
    the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Opening message">
    Write the **opening message** visitors see when they start the chat. Use it to
    greet them and hint at what the assistant can help with. Leave it off and
    Linkzee writes a persuasive opening for you automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tone of voice">
    Pick a **tone** — friendly, professional, or lively. The assistant keeps that
    voice in every reply, so it always sounds like you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Facts about your business">
    Add the **facts about your business** the assistant should use: what you sell,
    delivery and shipping details, opening hours, policies — anything you want it
    to know. The more you include, the more it can help.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Advanced personalization (optional)

Your assistant already works great with just the steps above. When you want to
tailor it to your store, open **Advanced personalization** — it's tucked away and
closed by default, so there's nothing extra you *have* to fill in. Two optional
free-text fields:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="How it should serve" icon="user-pen">
    Describe the manner you want, beyond the tone — for example: *"Serve like an
    experienced, warm advisor. Simple language, no jargon, and always praise the
    customer's choice."*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rules and preferences" icon="list-check">
    Give your own rules the assistant should always follow — for example: *"Never
    show all the times — offer only 2 at a time to create a sense of exclusivity"*
    or *"Always mention free shipping over R$99."*
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

There are ready-made examples you can tap to add to the rules — a quick way to get
started.

<Note>
  These preferences shape how your assistant behaves, but they never override its
  safety rules: it still won't invent products, prices, or booking times, and it
  won't promise things it can't do.
</Note>

## It only knows what you tell it

Your assistant answers **only** from the facts you provide. It never makes things
up, so if a visitor asks about something you haven't covered, it won't invent an
answer. That keeps every reply accurate and on-brand.

To go further, you can also feed it documents to answer from — see the
**knowledge base**.

<Note>
  Write the facts as if you were briefing a new salesperson on their first day.
  The clearer and more specific you are, the better your assistant sells for you.
</Note>
